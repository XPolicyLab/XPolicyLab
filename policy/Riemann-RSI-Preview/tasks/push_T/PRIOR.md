Use the declared contact workflow for this mode. Qualify the
source T and printed goal from current RGB and independent stereo
surfaces before commands. Keep the original nearest legal own-IK
branch, Cartesian descent, bounded contact servo, settling, release
and return-home. Reobserve a free object after every release.

An own-wrist attachment is a hypothesis, not object pose or physical
contact evidence. A wrist crossbar angle is only relative image
feedback. No layouts, object templates or scoring state are policy
inputs. Do not delegate to WAM/Qwen or rerun resolver selection.
