# Low-contact block alignment

## Applicability

Use only when the actual instruction asks to align blocks and the current RGB shows the movable block faces and a long neutral pusher. This is qualitative procedure knowledge, not scene evidence or official progress.

## Staged decisions

- Establish current public pusher and block geometry, using a bounded open-wrist view when necessary.
- Open both grippers, then approach the rear of the pusher with the same downward fingertip orientation while closing.
- Lower both fingertips and move both own arms along one common, slow pusher-normal displacement. Do not individually lift and rotate the blocks.
- Open the grippers after the sweep, then return both own arms to the observed episode-home joint targets.
- Keep the pusher motion low and quasi-static; robot arrival alone does not prove alignment, lack of lift or official completion.

## Evidence and recovery

- Current surface targets come only from public RGB rays and own robot observations. Do not substitute remembered scene geometry or asset dimensions.
- A prepared own command recipe can qualify the next stage without repeating unrelated perception after occlusion. It is not a physical contact certificate.
- Both arms are required for a paired sweep; do not silently continue with just one failed plan.
- Preserve irreversible stage receipts and do not replay a partly issued sweep. Missing evidence permits only bounded reinspection or learned recovery.
