# Bounded absolute EE motion

`ee_motion.py` provides `move_ee(arm, target, gripper, max_steps, tolerance, min_steps)` for the dual ARX X5. Include the file explicitly through env.py. Targets are world metres and scalar-first unit quaternions; gripper is 0 closed to 1 open. It holds the other arm at its measured pose and gripper command, measures position and quaternion agreement after every native step, and stops after two converged measurements, termination, truncation, or the supplied step cap. Set max_steps within the live native budget. Set min_steps for physical settling when changing a gripper command; command state alone does not verify a grasp.

Preconditions: reachable collision-free endpoint and safe intervening motion. The native controller is not a collision planner. Stage approach, descent, lift, and transport separately. This helper does not perceive objects or certify grasps. A final error above tolerance means the caller must inspect the state and revise the target.

Evidence: observation 000002 reached right pose (0.12, -0.28, 1.03) with quaternion (0.5, -0.5, 0.5, 0.5) to under 0.1 mm position error after 24 native steps. This quaternion points the gripper downward in this scene. The helper converged in 4-6 steps for the reachable moves in observations 000004-000011. Contact-limited motion in 000006 used its step cap and reported residual error. Only this scene has been explored.

`translate_ee(arm, xyz, gripper, max_steps=60, increment=0.008, tolerance=0.002)` preserves the measured starting quaternion and moves along the translation using a bounded increment from each latest measured position. Increment is metres per native action, not an exact physical speed. It holds the other arm and stops on convergence, five non-progress steps, termination, truncation, or budget. Use for already-grasped objects when endpoint jumps visibly rotate the object in the fingers. It does not correct object slip. Observation 000023 validated translation over 0.36 m in 53 actions, reaching within 0.2 mm. The poorly held disc slipped, so this validates arm motion only, not grasp stability. `move_ee` also stops after four near-constant residual-error steps once min_steps is satisfied; observed no-motion IK rejection stopped in five steps in 000014.

Final successful evidence: observations 000028-000032 used `translate_ee` to lift and carry a centred downward grasp, align over a pad, and retreat after release. Carry at increment=0.006 reached (-0.084,-0.15,1.00) within 0.1 mm in 51 actions; final approach at 0.005 reached the release pose within 0.2 mm in 38 actions. Both retained the tile flat. Official success followed release and the return-home command. See `flat_tile_transfer.md` for the reusable procedure. Require max_steps >= 1, tolerance > 0, increment > 0, and max_steps within the live remaining allowance. Callers must inspect residual error and terminal flags before starting another stage; these helpers do not turn a stall into success.
