# Pull and release a hinged panel

Include `servo_pose.py` and `linear_transport.py` first. `fold_hinged_panel` is a parameterized contact procedure for a lid whose reachable panel edge can be straddled by the jaws. The caller supplies the arm, contact pose, a list of `(world_xyz, action_cap)` pull waypoints, a clear retreat pose, and the current action budget. Positions are metres; poses use scalar-first quaternions. The helper checks its worst-case total action allocation before moving, verifies approach tracking, closes at the measured pose, pulls through feedback-controlled increments, releases at the final measured pose, and retreats. A stop flag may mean task termination, tracking failure, failed approach or insufficient budget; it never certifies panel closure.

Preconditions: identify the hinge, edge and sweep direction from current images; use a reachable yaw that aligns the jaw opening across panel thickness. Clear the sweep and retreat path. The procedure may stall as the panel contacts its base; release and inspection are necessary. It does not infer hinge angle, confirm latching, guarantee object clearance, or decide that the overall task succeeded.
