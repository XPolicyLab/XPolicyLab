of the Bagel-Robotics-1 approach. These reused source components retain the MIT
notice in LICENSE. The method contribution combines structured pruning and
recovery training with effective selection of task-critical data, as described
in the policy README.

licenses/Apache-2.0.txt. Source attribution is retained in individual modules
where present. Source hashes and packaging changes accompany the adapter in
