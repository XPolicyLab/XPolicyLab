RSIWAM: methodological review note (2026-11-02)
Model category: WAM with imitation-learning adaptation; no external online Agent
or task-based policy/checkpoint router is claimed.

Motivation and contribution to be reviewed
The research problem is adapting corrective behavior in off-demonstration states
while retaining a pretrained policy's ordinary behavior. The submitted system
freezes its foundation and world features, uses an observation-conditioned gate
to select an action-attention residual, and trains Action LoRA while retaining
the fixed residual branch's derivative with respect to action hidden states.
Thus the LoRA update accounts for the composed action generator when the gate
opens; it is not trained against a detached recovery-branch input. This coupling
and the explicitly separated normal/handback/Agent-proxy supervision are the
methodological claims presented for review. The names and component combination
alone are not offered as novelty evidence or as a new pretrained foundation.

At inference, the gated residual and Action LoRA form one fixed system. The
pretrained VPP2 world/action foundation, original RecoverWAM parent seed20261032
and Action LoRA seed20261033 step400 retain their published identities. All tasks
use the same weights, hard gate threshold0.9, ten denoising steps, 32-action
horizon and 24 actions per replan. Normal/recovery balancing is a training
sampling policy, not a task-dependent selection of inference checkpoints.

Training and limitations
The frozen original bridge predates success133 adaptation. During the subsequent
400-step Action LoRA stage, base and bridge weights remain frozen; q/v LoRA is
trained using the existing masked action flow-matching objective. Agent repair
and finish action labels are screened forward-kinematics proxies, not certified
execution-equivalent labels. A successful source trajectory does not make every
prefix action a correct recovery target. The report and training supplement
state the exact split, masks, label scopes and continuation limitations.

Existing empirical evidence
The public technical-report draft freezes an earlier46-scene comparison. A later
2026-11-02 development snapshot contains143 aligned initial-state cases across
35 tasks:135 exact raw initial-state matches and8 asset-root-only differences
with matched referenced asset hashes. VPP2 succeeds46/143 (32.17%), ordinary
Action LoRA46/143 (32.17%), and RSIWAM51/143 (35.66%). Mean episode Score/100 is
40.00,39.37,42.24 respectively. Against VPP2, RSIWAM has12 failure-to-success and
7 success-to-failure changes; the net5 equals the success-count difference.
This is a completion-selected partial development subset, not the official
three-seed result or a certified within-episode recovery rate. Cross-hardware
RGB/dynamics equivalence remains unverified. No statistical significance claim,
architectural priority claim or comprehensive no-regression claim is made.

The current full local campaign uses the same RSIWAM candidate and native
RoboDojo task rules. Its official summarize output is separately attached;
dimension-balanced partial summaries and development pooled episode scores
are not interchangeable. Official eligibility and hidden-layout acceptance
must be determined by the organizers. Further ablations and recovery-state
criteria would be required to attribute a specific recovery benefit.
