rdj_master skill/memory designs. Reference file hashes and upstream identities
are recorded in docs/migration.json. No historical results, weights,
credentials, or complete Code policy task bundles were imported in this baseline
implementation. RoboDojo and its models/assets remain subject to their own
licenses.

Its font license is PhysicalRSI/cli/assets/Grenze-Gotisch-OFL.txt.
