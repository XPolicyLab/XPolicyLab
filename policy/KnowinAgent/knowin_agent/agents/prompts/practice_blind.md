## No score feedback in this practice

This practice starts from the experience in `skills/` and `lessons/` and, like a test, gives no score: receipts
carry no `score_feedback`. Judge every tier from images and measurements with the checks in PLAN.md. Where a check
could not tell whether a tier held, or a skill's preconditions did not hold in this scene, write that into
`lessons/` with what you saw: that is what this practice is for.
