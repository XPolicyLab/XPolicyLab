play_Xylophone: native action limit 500. Binary: 100 or 0.

Success is a sequence checked in order, one step at a time:

1. the mallet's beat point is inside bar 0's region and 1-3.6 cm above bar 0's hit point;
2. the mallet is lifted at least 2.5 cm from where it struck;
3. the same strike-then-lift for bar 1, then bars 2 .. 6;
4. a final strike on bar 7.

Bars must be struck in order 0 to 7, and the mallet must lift between strikes; a strike on the wrong
bar does not count and does not reset progress. There is no return-to-origin requirement.
