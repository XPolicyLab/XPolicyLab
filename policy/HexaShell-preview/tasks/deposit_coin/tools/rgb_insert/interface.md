`robo upright_insert --top=x,y,z --opening=x,y,z --end1=x,y,z --end2=x,y,z --diameter D --thickness T [--mode preview|move]`
Grasps a thin upright circular part, lifts, transfers into a measured narrow aperture, checks release, retreats and attempts both-arm home.
World meters: top is exposed crest center; opening is horizontal jaw direction across the faces; end1/end2 are aperture endpoints at mouth height.
--diameter and --thickness are REQUIRED and have no default: measure both in this episode with `rgb_geometry` (diameter: circle through ≥3 triangulated rim points, or length_m of two opposite rim points; thickness: length_m across the two face edges). Missing values fail with "diameter and thickness are required". Generic bounds only: diameter 0.01–0.10 m, thickness 0.0002–0.02 m. --depth 0.012 accepts 0.010–0.6×diameter m. There is no --offset: the held center is searched from the measured crest minus the measured radius and must be fitted from RGB after the lift.
--pitch -1 selects automatic 0°/60° acquisition; explicit 0–60° accepted. Default --mode preview validates and fits without motion or reachability testing.
Coordinates/diameter are search hints: calibrated RGB silhouettes must resolve crest, face direction and radius within ±25% of the diameter hint; fresh wrist/multi-view evidence must agree with initial accepted silhouettes before contact.
Move may perform bounded unloaded view translation, adds six closure-dwell steps, lifts 0.12 m, and checks RGB retention through bounded transfer. Commanded aperture is not measured convergence; paths are not collision checked.
An unresolved held circle stops closed (held_offset_unresolved_keep_closed). One source-visible empty retry is time-bounded. Release requires wrist support and ≥10 mm inferred penetration; ≥30 steps are reserved for home. The post-release check searches a square of half-width (aperture length/2 + diameter) around the mouth.
Returns plan_ok/plan_fail_reason, executed, active_arm, poses, source/refinement diagnostics, stages, retention/insertion checks, held_offset_local, released, placement and home_status. Visual disappearance does not prove completion.
Invalid geometry, unresolved/conflicting appearance, planning/tracking failure or insufficient time fails; executed failures attempt both-arm return without opening.
