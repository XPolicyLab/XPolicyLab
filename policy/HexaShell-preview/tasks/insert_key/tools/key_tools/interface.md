`robo key_scene [--key_u U --key_v V] [--hole_u U --hole_v V] [--opening_z Z] [--relay_x X --relay_y Y]`: motion-free RGB geometry of the part, the dark opening and both grips, the part sizes measured so far, and IK route estimates; pixel hints are paired nonnegative head-image integers. Without Z the opening is reported only as a head pixel and no routes are estimated; relay routes need X, Y.
`robo key_opening_z [--hole_u U --hole_v V] [--camera2 cam_left_wrist|cam_right_wrist [--u2 U2 --v2 V2]]`: motion-free; triangulates the dark opening seen at the same moment in obs/head.png and in a wrist image and prints `opening_z`, the world z of the top face around it. A wrist camera must look down at the opening first (e.g. `robo point <arm> down --open x`, then `robo move <arm>` until it is in that wrist image), else it fails with opening_not_in_two_views. Explicit pixels (U2, V2 in that wrist image) replace the automatic match.
`robo key_pick left|right [--mode move|preview]`: refine a broad-end grasp, descend, unload table pressure, close, lift and check retention. The first pick measures the part's length, ring width and ring height from the head view and its wrist view above the part; if those views disagree it stops open above it (key_height_unmeasured: run key_scene, then pick again).
`robo key_relay left|right --x X --y Y [--mode move|preview]`: place from the named holder at X, Y, release, withdraw and return stable pickup geometry. X, Y are required: world metres of a free table spot both arms reach, measured from the images (a head pixel on bare table intersected with z = table_z from `robo table_plane`); there is no default spot. Unresolved settling invalidates the target and gates pickup.
`robo key_pivot left|right --opening_z Z [--mode move|preview]`: preflight competing RGB directions and bounded staging poses, then rotate closed about the contact toward vertical; remeasure each <=15-degree leg, at most ten legs; stop at tracking error >3 mm or >2 degrees.
`robo key_align left|right --opening_z Z [--mode move|preview]`: refine the opening from wrist RGB and align the held tip 1 cm above its centre at height Z; requires verified upright context.
`robo key_insert left|right --opening_z Z [--mode move|preview] [--turn DEG | --dir cw|ccw]`: segmented descent until contact or the measured travel limit below Z, with optional axial rotation; DEG is -180..180, cw/ccw means -90/+90 degrees.
Z is required wherever shown: the world z of the top face around the dark opening, measured in this episode with key_opening_z (0.5-30 cm above the table); nothing assumes it. Part sizes are measured from the images in the episode; nothing assumes them either.
Motion defaults to move; preview plans without motion. Geometry uses calibrated RGB rays and an image-measured table plane; positions are world metres.
Returns plan_ok, plan_fail_reason, failure_class, executed, stages, sim_time_left_s and available geometry, uncertainty and route costs; IK estimates do not certify clearance or retention. Missing required arguments are refused with what to measure.
Invalid arguments, missing/ambiguous geometry, unverified retention, unreachable poses, contact stalls or insufficient time return failure, possibly after partial motion.
