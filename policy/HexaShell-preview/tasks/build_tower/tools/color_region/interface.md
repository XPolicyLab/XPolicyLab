`robo region --u U --v V [--color_tolerance 0.12] [--gap 0.015]` measures a connected RGB-depth region at a head-image pixel, including inclined geometry; no motion or action-step cost.
`robo regions [--color_tolerance 0.12] [--gap 0.015] [--min_chroma 0.15]` inventories regions without a seed or horizontal restriction; also free.
Color_tolerance is normalized RGB distance (0.01–0.3); gap is neighboring 3D separation (0.001–0.04 m); min_chroma (0–1) filters (max RGB-min RGB)/max RGB, with zero including neutral colors.
Returns plan_ok/plan_fail_reason and world visible_center, percentile bounds, height_range_m, median_rgb, plane_normal/plane_rms_m, samples, pixel, upper_band_center/samples, footprint_extent_m/long_direction_xy and upper_band_heading_deg/span_m. Inventory uses region_columns/region_rows rounded to 4 decimals, sorted by height, plus visible_region_count and rejected_regions.
Height_range_m spans the 1st–99th percentiles; upper_band_center is median XYZ within 0.003 m below the 99th-percentile height, null below five samples. Footprint extents are 1st–99th percentile XY principal spans; long direction is null for covariance ratio <=1.2.
Upper-band heading is counterclockwise from world +X modulo 180 degrees in [-90,90); span is the 5th–95th percentile projected extent. Both are null below eight samples, covariance ratio <=3 or span <0.006 m. A ridge, high edge or partial face may produce the contour.
Fails for invalid arguments/observations, dark seeds, insufficient samples, oversized regions or an empty inventory. Inventory omits dark/tiny/oversized regions; shadows and depth gaps split, touching colors merge, and plane fits can span multiple faces. Visible centers and directions do not certify grasp poses, hidden dimensions, identity, contact or completion.
