`robo mate-pair ARM --source JSON --target '[[x,y,z],[x,y,z]]' [--source_frame world|tcp] [--source_axis '[x,y,z]'] [--target_axis '[x,y,z]'] [--clearance M] [--depth M] [--tolerance M] [--wrist_lift_deg D] [--correspondence ordered|either] [--park_other 0|1] [--park_clearance M] [--compact 0|1] [--orient_step_deg D]`
Aligns rigid feature pairs with measured feature-to-TCP offset compensation; ARM=left|right, coordinates/distances in meters. Source is two current points or `{points,axis,frame,arm}`; bundle axis/frame override flags and TCP arm must match ARM. Local captures require unchanged rigid attachment.
`source_frame=world`; tcp transforms points/axis through the current TCP. Target points/axis always use world coordinates. Axes default [0,0,-1] in their respective frames, perpendicular to pairs. Target spacing is reconciled to source spacing only within tolerance, retaining target midpoint/direction.
`clearance=0.015` (0..0.08), `depth=0` (0..0.03 beyond target), `tolerance=0.002` (0.0005..0.005). `wrist_lift_deg=0` (0..25) permits calibrated midpoint tilt bounded by tip/shaft geometry and half the remaining tolerance; advance follows target_axis.
`correspondence=ordered` preserves order; either permits endpoint exchange. Compact stepped routes compare calibrated wrist travel and, when planner is available, sequentially preflight both permitted routes before motion. Preflight covers descent/final advance; it does not verify collisions or physical joint limits.
`park_other=1` requires the other hand commanded open; homes it, then permits <=0.15 m outward lift to satisfy calibrated segment gap `park_clearance=0.12` (0..0.2). Zero clearance means home only; park_other=0 omits parking. Segment clearance is not whole-arm clearance.
`compact=1` combines small rotations/transfer; larger rotations use elevated coordinated increments then axial descent <=20 mm. Geometric plane-clearance checks gate compact routes. `orient_step_deg=20` (5..45, or 0 for one rotation) bounds rotation increments with calibrated interpolation checks; compact=0 separates orientation/transfer.
Advances <=2 mm; checks combined predicted tip/shaft path errors against tolerance. One endpoint exchange may follow motionless orientation/transfer IK rejection; no contact retries or exchange after partial failed motion. Stops on invalid geometry, planner/calibration error, tracking/contact or exhausted budget; never releases.
Returns plan_ok/plan_fail_reason, stage details, resolved source geometry, correspondence, preflight_attempts/kinematic_path_preflight_passed when applicable, increment counts, applied tilt, feature/shaft errors, predicted_points_world/predicted_depth_m and grip_unchanged/seat_verified=false.
After executed motion fails, cancels active targets at measured joints and reports target_cancelled/cancel_error. Rigid attachment is assumed; tracking and predicted depth do not verify physical engagement. Planned action-step estimates exclude parking/settling.
