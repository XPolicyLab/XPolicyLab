`robo move_feature <left|right> --u U --v V --x X --y Y --z Z [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--yaw 0] [--clearance .06]` positions a selected attached surface using calibrated depth and its TCP offset; hand must be commanded closed. Pixels share the selected camera (default head); distances are metres. Clearance 0..0.20; zero is direct, positive adds raised positioning. Yaw -90..90 degrees; feature reach/displacement <=.50 m.
`robo stroke_feature <left|right>` takes the same inputs plus `--end_x EX --end_y EY --end_z EZ [--retract .05] [--other_lift 0] [--corridor_radius .03]`: raised positioning, straight stroke and vertical retraction. Clearance/retract .025..0.20, length .02..0.50, height change <=.04; radius .01..0.15. Other_lift 0..0.20 raises the opposite hand first and leaves it raised; neither gripper changes. TCP paths require .16 m opposite-hand separation.
`robo inspect_stroke <left|right>` takes stroke inputs and performs free geometric preflight, returning planned_motions, preflight_only=true, motion_executed=false, ik_checked=false; execution remeasures. Preview does not verify IK, contact or retention.
Stroke contact alternative: omit z/end_z; supply `--contact_u CU --contact_v CV --plane_z PZ [--gap .002]`. A separate rigid contact point sets reference heights so contact reaches PZ+gap (gap 0..0.02). Optional `--edge_u2 EU --edge_v2 EV` makes U,V and EU,EV unordered edge endpoints: XY locates their midpoint; automatic yaw sets the edge perpendicular to travel. Requires width .025..0.50, height difference <=.015, yaw=0 and no direction inputs; returns edge_width_m and edge_yaw_deg.
Optional `--u2 U2 --v2 V2 --axis_x AX --axis_y AY --axis_z AZ` with yaw=0 aligns a directed pair by shortest rotation; spacing .025..0.50, correction <=120 degrees, twist unconstrained. Move-only `--plane_u3 PU --plane_v3 PV` also levels a plane to +Z; target axis must be horizontal, initial tilt <=60 degrees, triangle altitude >=.02 and sine angle >=.25. Clearance >=.025; fixed-TCP turn adds measured triangle arc clearance, total raise <=.20. Returns plane geometry, alignment_pivot and alignment_lift_m; unseen thickness and joint interpolation remain unchecked.
Stroke `--via_x VX --via_y VY` adds one raised XY waypoint for the clearance anchor (contact in contact mode, reference otherwise); each leg .01..0.50, total <=.75. Live depth sets anchor height >=.015 above visible corridor surfaces, including carried geometry; empty depth or required clearance >.20 fails. Paired-edge radius is at least half edge width. Returns transit_segments, effective_clearance_m, transit_limiting_pixel/world and anchor endpoints; turning arcs, descent and unseen space are not certified.
Excessive-clearance failure may return up to three transit_waypoint_candidates with via_x/via_y, clearance and per-leg evidence. Candidates are depth-only hints requiring explicit selection and fresh inspect_stroke; they exclude IK, TCP separation and complete visibility. No candidate means no qualifying sampled route, not proof of infeasibility.
Motion returns plan_ok, plan_fail_reason, stages, source_camera, feature_measurements, target_tcp_world and predicted_feature_world; strokes add stroke_start_world/end_world, stroke_end_tcp_world, minimum_tcp_separation_m and other_raised. Contact/direction modes include measured and predicted geometry. Invalid inputs/depth, clipping, pose error or exhaustion stop; hand may remain low. Motions cost simulation time. Stationary stroke IK failure identifying a failed waypoint permits one preceding-waypoint prefix attempt and retraction: partial_stroke=true remains plan_ok=false, with completion/retraction fields.
Initial-raise attachment_check stops on observed stationary source plus empty predicted position. Later placement_checks stop when calibrated depth is >=.018 behind the rigid prediction, returning relocalization_required=true. Missing/occluded evidence is unknown; occupancy never confirms retention. Mixed-depth pixels require a connected planar center layer; identity, rigidity, contact and clear full geometry remain caller assumptions.
`robo entry_path --u U --v V --u2 U2 --v2 V2 --inside_u IU --inside_v IV --source_u SU --source_v SV [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--margin .02] [--backoff .04]` freely measures a path through a finite level entry: first pair is entry, inside sets inward side/end, source sets exterior point. Margin .005..0.10 is path half-width; backoff .01..0.15 extends behind source.
Entry returns plan_ok/plan_fail_reason, start_xy/end_xy, direction_world, transverse_axis_world, measured points, crossing and clearance; no Z. Invalid depth, unsupported boundary fits, geometry, length or insufficient margin fail; margin failure returns entry_center_shift_xy without executable endpoints. Interior space, identity and full footprint remain unverified.
