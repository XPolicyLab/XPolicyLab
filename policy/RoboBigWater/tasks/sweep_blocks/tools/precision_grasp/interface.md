`robo metric_point --u U --v V [--u2 U2 --v2 V2] [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r]` freely projects selected depth pixels; returns surface_world, optional second_world, axis_deg, depth spread, methods/errors, local_support, height_difference_m and inclination_deg.
Mixed depth requires a connected planar center layer; missing/nonplanar depth fails. Valid first-point evidence survives second-point failure. Local support reports nearby horizontal height, relative point height and fit evidence, or unknown; it does not establish underside/contact. Failed pixels may return up to three reselection_candidates within 8 pixels/.025 m, requiring explicit fresh selection; identity is unverified and coordinates are never substituted.
`robo grasp_at <left|right> --x X --y Y --z Z --axis DEGREES [--inset .006] [--clearance .08] [--lift .06] [--tilt 0] [--tilt_axis DEGREES]` performs raised orientation, opening, approach, descent, closure and lift. XYZ is a visible surface; inset subtracts world z. Axis runs from +x toward +y; closure is perpendicular to feature and approach.
Metres: inset 0..0.03, clearance .03..0.20, lift 0..0.20; tilt -45..45 degrees toward tilt_axis (default axis; 90 means +y). Preflight/live TCP separation is >=.16 m from a commanded-closed opposite hand, .20 m otherwise; not full collision checking.
Head/receiving-wrist depth must confirm XYZ within .008 m before motion/descent; bounded refresh adjusts descent. Closure needs direct evidence or foreground occlusion plus two unchanged connected sections captured before descent (inferred=true). Contradiction/missing evidence stops before closure with relocalization_required; no donor release.
Observed support checks .04 m finger half-span and .003 m clearance before motion/descent; contradiction returns support_clearance, measured height and maximum inset. Missing support stays unknown; reachability and contact remain unverified.
Lifts >=.025 m track connected RGB features: two following witnesses set grasp_verified=true; two stationary witnesses fail. Depth sections or selected-surface evidence can reject a stationary source with visible empty space at its predicted lifted position. Missing, conflicting or occluded evidence remains unknown; depth alone never verifies attachment. Returns visual_lift_check, depth_check and selected_surface_check; no extra motion.
`robo probe_hold <left|right> --u U --v V [--distance .03]` lifts a closed hand .025..0.05 m and tests head-image RGB/depth co-motion; selected feature must be unique, textured and >=.065 m from TCP. Leaves hand raised; neither gripper changes.
Probe returns grasp_verified, feature error, motion and match score; static, occluded, ambiguous features or invalid depth fail. Evidence applies to the selected feature, not grip strength.
Motion commands return plan_ok, plan_fail_reason and stage evidence; stop on invalid input, planning, clipping, pose error or exhaustion. Motion costs simulation time; no blind retries or automatic release. Occupancy does not establish surface identity or retention.
