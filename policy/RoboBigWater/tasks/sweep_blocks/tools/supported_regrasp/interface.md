## supported_regrasp / rest_feature
`robo supported_regrasp <left|right> --u U --v V --u2 U2 --v2 V2 --support_z Z --thickness T [--clearance .04] [--lift .04] [--tilt 0]`
`robo rest_feature <left|right> --u U --v V --u2 U2 --v2 V2 --support_z Z --thickness T`
Both lower the donor vertically to caller-specified support, check observed surfaces, release, withdraw .08 m along negative approach, then retreat outward in x by at least .14 m, ending at least .25 m laterally from the release TCP and both predicted feature points; retreat above .40 m fails preflight. Returns retreat_preflight with target_tcp_world and lateral_clearance_m; this does not certify full arm collision clearance.
U,V and U2,V2 select two visible top-surface points on one nearly horizontal rigid feature, .025..25 m apart, with consistent depth; Z is support height and T is surface-to-bottom distance (.004..05 m).
Both accept `--contact_u CU --contact_v CV [--contact_offset 0]`: a head-depth surface on the same rigid item sets lowering independently of T; offset (0..05 m) is its vertical distance to the lowest underside. Underside must be below the tracked pair; contact within .40 m of TCP. Returns lowering_geometry in stages with source_contact_world, source_bottom_z, lowering_dz_m; checks contact depth occupancy within .008 m before release. Lowest-point identity and unseen geometry remain caller assumptions.
Depth preflight rejects Z contradicted by a broad local horizontal surface, returning measured_support_z in stages; unavailable evidence is unknown. Caller supplies a clear supporting footprint; contact and attachment remain assumptions. Donor must be commanded closed, opposite hand open; descent is limited to .20 m.
`supported_regrasp` requires unique texture, tracks settled features and acquires with the opposite hand; requires 10 simulation seconds remaining.
`rest_feature` needs no texture: checks depth occupancy at predicted pixels, leaves the opposite hand untouched, returns relocalization_required=true; requires 4 simulation seconds remaining. Occupancy does not verify identity; settled coordinates require fresh measurement.
Distances are metres: clearance .03..12, lift .02..10; tilt -45..45 degrees. Returns plan_ok, plan_fail_reason, stages, donor_released; grasp_verified=false.
Stops on invalid inputs, inconsistent depth/tracking, planning, clipping, pose error or episode end; no retries. Failures after release report donor_released=true. Motion consumes simulation time.
