Common motion syntax: `robo COMMAND {left|right} --u U --v V [--patch N] [--max_seconds S]`; full-resolution head pixels select a depth surface. patch=21 (odd 9..61), max_seconds=6 (1..10); distances in meters, coordinates/offsets in world axes. Only the selected arm moves. Held-surface commands require closed jaws and a selection within 0.18 m of TCP; texture or unique depth-filtered context must support tracking.
`robo pixel_probe --u U --v V [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r]` returns surface_world, timestamp, plan_ok and plan_fail_reason; default camera=head, no motion or budget cost.
`visual_grasp` adds `[--preset down|down45|forward] [--open x|y|z] [--inset M] [--clearance M] [--lift M]`: defaults down,y,0.015,0.08,0.12; bounds inset=-0.05..0.06, clearance=0.03..0.18, lift=0.04..0.20. Tracks, clears local depth geometry, intercepts, closes, lifts and checks retention for 0.4 s; inset follows approach, clearance opposes it. One contact correction and bounded atomic IK recovery; returns grasp_verified, observed_lift_m, transit_height_m and tracking/recovery diagnostics.
`visual_lift` adds `[--dx M] [--dy M] [--dz M]`: defaults 0,0,0.12; dx/dy=-0.3..0.3, dz=0.04..0.20. Direct fixed-orientation displacement, 0.24 s retention check, observed_lift_m >= dz-0.005 required; leaves jaws closed.
`visual_reposition` adds `--x X --y Y --z Z`: direct fixed-orientation move of the selected surface to X,Y,Z, preserving its TCP offset; checks retention for 0.24 s and leaves jaws closed. No clearance detour.
`visual_transfer` adds `--x X --y Y --z Z [--clearance M] [--retreat M]`: clearance=0.05 (0.02..0.15), retreat=0.10 (0.04..0.20). Raises by clearance, translates while ascending to max(source Z,Z)+clearance, lowers the selected surface to X,Y,Z, checks retention for 0.24 s, opens and withdraws vertically at fixed orientation.
`visual_release` adds `[--retreat M]`: retreat=0.10 (0.04..0.20); checks retention for 0.24 s, opens in place and withdraws vertically at fixed orientation.
`visual_align` adds `--ref_u U --ref_v V --dz M [--dx M] [--dy M]`: reference head pixels define destination=reference_world+[dx,dy,dz]; dx=dy=0, all offsets=-0.3..0.3. Source/reference separation must be >=0.03 m. Direct fixed-orientation move with 0.24 s checks before/after; drift <=0.01 m and alignment error <=0.015 m required; leaves jaws closed. Returns alignment_verified, alignment_error_m, reference_world, reference_drift_m, destination_world.
`visual_place` adds the same reference/offset arguments as align and `[--clearance M] [--retreat M]`: defaults 0.03,0.10; bounds 0.02..0.15,0.04..0.20. Raises, translates while ascending above source/reference/destination heights plus clearance, lowers, verifies alignment, releases and withdraws; checks retention/reference drift before, throughout and after transfer. Returns alignment and release flags; other arm stays unchanged.
Motion results include plan_ok, plan_fail_reason, stages, surface_world and tracking diagnostics. Release paths return release_commanded, released (measured opening >=0.90), withdrawn (TCP reached retreat), retention_verified (pre-release only); no detachment or final resting-position guarantee. Motion costs action steps; time limits checked between primitives can overrun by one primitive; release reserves 1.12 s before opening.
Invalid inputs/depth, unresolved ambiguity/occlusion, workspace/IK failure, TCP miss or retention slip >0.015 m fail. Failures after closure leave jaws closed until a release stage; withdrawal can fail after release. No collision-free-path guarantee; align/reposition use direct paths and relative commands do not chase drifting references.
