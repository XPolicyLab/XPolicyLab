`robo pixel_point --u U --v V [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r]`: calibrated world XYZ at one depth pixel; no motion; region_verified=false; fails on invalid pixel, depth or calibration.
`robo surface_frame --u U --v V [--camera head] [--radius 40] [--color_tol 45]`: no-motion connected thin-region geometry, endpoints, axis, dimensions, broad contact and pixel support; fails on truncation or invalid geometry.
`robo visual_pinch left|right --u U --v V [--camera head] [--radius 40] [--color_tol 45] [--contact broad|pixel] [--sink 0] [--opening 0.35] [--clearance 0.07] [--lift 0.08] [--yaw DEGREES]`: one vertical pinch and visually checked lift.
Pinch bounds: sink [-0.006,0.012], clearance [0.04,0.15], lift [0.06,0.15] metres; opening [0.25,1]; yaw is the world-XY jaw axis, default perpendicular to region length. Unsupported or excessively low pixel contacts fail before motion.
`robo transfer_frame left|right --u U --v V [--camera head] [--radius 40] [--color_tol 45] [--approach down|horizontal] [--presentation auto|none] [--opening 0.55] [--sink 0.004]`: no-motion donor/receiver geometry, waypoints and witness checks; motion_checked=false, no IK or collision certification.
`robo visual_transfer left|right --u U --v V [--camera head] [--radius 40] [--color_tol 45] [--approach down|horizontal] [--presentation auto|none] [--opening 0.55] [--sink 0.004]`: positional arm is donor; bounded presentation, receiver approach, staged closure, donor release and visually checked 35 mm lift.
Transfer bounds: downward donor, selected surface 40–120 mm horizontally from donor TCP; height difference at most min(60 mm,12 mm + horizontal distance × tan(30°)) for down, 25 mm for horizontal; opening [0.2,0.8], effective down minimum 0.55; sink [-0.006,0.008] metres, positive below surface.
All camera arguments accept head|wrist_l|wrist_r. Radius is an initial integer window [5,largest image dimension]; measurement remains bounded to 120 mm. Color tolerance is [5,100], with transfer floor 45. Auto presentation may translate/yaw donor; none disables it.
Failed region measurement may return pickup_selection; verified pickup may return transfer_selection; failed transfer preflight may return selection_help. These are bounded camera/pixel hints: identity_verified=false, motion_checked=false, search_complete=false; empty results do not establish absence.
Motion feedback includes plan_ok, plan_fail_reason, stages and grasp_verified=true|false|null; transfer includes donor_released, effective aperture, closure schedule and fit diagnostics. donor_released=false records no opening command, not retention; null verification is inconclusive.
Commands fail on invalid arguments, geometry, insufficient time, inaccurate tracking or ambiguous/lost visual evidence. Transfer permits bounded evidence-gated fitting and correction; unresolved pre-release failures stop without opening donor and may reopen receiver. All internal motions consume action steps; measurements do not.
