`robo gripscan3d --rect '[x0,y0,x1,y1]' --angle A [--support_z Z] [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--min_width .012] [--max_width .065] [--span .012] [--finger_clearance .006]` surveys calibrated depth without motion or action steps.
Rect is an inclusive pixel rectangle; A is horizontal finger-opening direction in degrees. Widths/span are meters: .002 <= min_width < max_width <= .15, span .006–.04, finger_clearance 0–.03 (zero disables).
Omitted support_z estimates a broad horizontal border band, with one bounded surrounding-crop fallback; candidate bounds remain inside rect. Ambiguous support or inconsistent supplied elevation fails with diagnostics.
Returns up to five candidates: center_xy, surface_z, contact_z_estimate, height_above_support, width, width_range, angle, pixel, component, samples and height_samples; height uses the central 60% of each longitudinal third.
Contact Z is the support-to-top midpoint for a resting body, with no calibrated TCP offset. Returns effective_span (expanded for pixel spacing, at most .04 m), support_source and support_rect.
Ranks by balance_offset_fraction from a visible column-volume centroid, then height and width; insufficient triangle evidence falls back to length midpoint. Returns balance_reference, visible_balance_xy, balance_offset_m/fraction, longitudinal_offset_m/fraction and visible_longitudinal_span_m; longitudinal fields always refer to length midpoint. Hidden shape, density and crop truncation can bias ranking; mass/balance are unverified.
Rejects narrow, split, uneven, truncated or undersampled sections. Three raised depth samples in either lateral finger-clearance band veto a section, including neighbors outside rect; returns side_obstacle_samples and finger_clearance. Missing depth is unknown; collision_checked=false, reachability and retention unverified.
Returns plan_ok/plan_fail_reason; invalid arguments, missing depth or no qualifying section fail. No-section diagnostics include support provenance, effective_span, raised_samples, component_count, span/width/height-variation ranges and rejection_counts; null ranges mean unobserved, counts describe rejection stages rather than accepted candidates.
