`robo surface --u U --v V [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r]`: free, read-only RGB-D measurement of a compact colored patch within 12 px; default camera head.
Returns `plan_ok`, `plan_fail_reason`, `center_xy`, `top_z`, `extent_xyz`, `grasp_xyz`, `grasp_offset_xy`, `open_axis`, `cross_section_xy`, hue and pixel count; invalid pixels/depth or tiny, broad, depth-mixed patches fail.
`grasp_xyz` uses visible top height and thick material or an opposing-jaw midpoint; `grasp_offset_xy` is grasp minus footprint center, added to desired center xy to obtain destination TCP xy.
`robo transfer <left|right> --x X --y Y --z Z --to_x X --to_y Y --to_z Z [--open x|y] [--approach down|down45] [--clearance M] [--seat M] [--landing auto|off]`
Coordinates are absolute world TCP grasp/release positions in metres; active gripper must start open. Defaults: open=x, approach=down, clearance=0.045 (0.025–0.15), seat=0.003 (0–0.004), landing=auto; down45 tilts forward 45 degrees.
Observed seating lowers both endpoints equally by at most seat; landing=auto may additionally lower release 5–40 mm using validated support heights, otherwise preserves it. landing=off disables that release correction.
Executes bounded idle-arm clearing, orientation, approach, grasp, lift, carry, release and retraction; checks swept gripper clearance ≥0.14 m, not full arm-link collisions. Motion consumes simulation time.
Returns motion `stages`, `checks`, `released`, `plan_ok`/`plan_fail_reason`, and effective `grasp_xyz`/`release_xyz` on success. RGB-D evidence states are `visible_at_tcp`, `lost`, `unverified`; wrist-only carry evidence triggers a post-release placement check.
Missing source evidence after pre-contact retries returns `source_not_visible`; visible loss returns `visual_grasp_lost`. Unverified lift permits one 15 mm verification rise with full-route clearance; confirmed height is retained and checked after carry. Persistent uncertainty stops closed with `visual_grasp_unverified`; absent post-release confirmation returns `placement_unverified` after opening/retraction.
After unmoved carry IK refusal, may try one observed-clear lower route with fresh held-material confirmation; otherwise restores/releases at source once, reporting `plan_detail.source_release`. Other motion failures or episode end stop; obstructed/closed idle arm returns `idle_arm_obstructs_path`. No automatic regrasp.
