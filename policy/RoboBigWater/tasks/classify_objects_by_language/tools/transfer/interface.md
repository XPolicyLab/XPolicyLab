## Tool: transfer
`robo pick_place <left|right> --x X --y Y --z Z --tx X --ty Y --tz Z [options]` approaches, closes, lifts, carries, releases and retreats; adds `--approach down|down45` (down).
`robo place <left|right> --tx X --ty Y --tz Z [options]` transports an already closed gripper; `robo relay <left|right> --x X --y Y --z Z --hx X --hy Y --hz Z --tx X --ty Y --tz Z [options] [--receive_open x|y]` (x) transfers through a supported intermediate point with both arms and checked parking; opposite gripper must be open.
All coordinates are absolute world TCP points in meters. Options: `--open x|y` (x), `--carry down|down45|keep` (down45), `--clearance M` (0.10, range 0.03–0.30), `--transit_margin M` (0.03, 0.01–0.15), `--retreat M` (0.04, 0–0.20), `--tolerance M` (0.012, 0.001–0.03).
`robo transfer_check` accepts the same arguments as `pick_place`; returns nominal `preflight` reachability and `nominal_motion_steps`, or `preflight_unavailable`. `robo transfer_options <left|right|both>` takes those arguments with `--open auto|x|y` (auto), `--carry auto|down|down45|keep` (auto), `--approach auto|down|down45` (down); returns executable `candidates` ranked by nominal steps and `rejected` evidence, or `no_reachable_options`. Auto carry tries down/down45.
Both checks are read-only with no command/action cost; ranking excludes parking, settling and contacts. Motion commands cost one command plus internal action steps; nominal preflight excludes contacts and later depth raises, may select alternate approach ordering, and execution continues if planning is unavailable.
`clearance` controls vertical approach/lift; depth after carry rotation may raise transit using `transit_margin`, without lowering baseline height or changing release coordinates. Temporal foreground filtering is heuristic; missing/ambiguous depth can retain baseline height. This is not full collision checking.
Returns `plan_ok`, `plan_fail_reason`, `phase`, `released`, `stages`, preflight/clearance evidence and `grasp_verified=false`; relay adds `legs` and `intermediate_released`. Invalid inputs, reach/clipping/arrival errors, failed parking or episode end stop without retries; failed carry retains closure and release requires arrival.
Descent requires error <= min(tolerance, 0.003 m) before closure; `grasp_arrival` reports target, reached, signed `offset_m`, `error_m`, `tolerance_m`. Unchanged-source depth may stop with `source_not_lifted` after lift, rotation or translation; inconclusive evidence does not verify retention.
Relay intermediate depth may reject bare support (`intermediate_empty`) or an isolated surface center offset over 12 mm (`intermediate_misaligned`); returns `intermediate_check` evidence, without automatic coordinate correction. Missing/occluded/ambiguous evidence is inconclusive.
`inactive_clearance` checks opposite TCP proximity to nominal segments (100 mm); motion commands may park an open arm away from home, or fail `inactive_arm_occupied` for a closed one. Read-only checks report `park_required`; parking consumes action steps and is not collision-planned.
