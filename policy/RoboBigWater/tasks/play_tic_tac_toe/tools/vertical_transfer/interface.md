`robo {vertical-transfer,deposit-transfer} {left,right} --x X --y Y --z Z --to_x X --to_y Y --to_z Z [--clearance C] [--travel_z H] [--open {x,y}] [--approach {down,down45}]`
Absolute world TCP coordinates in meters; Z is orientation-dependent fingertip-center height. Requires open gripper and both arms home except an eligible corrected-entry continuation. Descends, closes, lifts, travels horizontally, lowers, opens and retracts; vertical-transfer also homes and awaits visual departure/return, while deposit-transfer leaves both pending.
Defaults: open=x, approach=down, clearance=0.03 (range 0.03–0.15); travel_z overrides max(Z,to_Z)+clearance but must be >=0.03 above both endpoints. Orientation stays fixed. vertical-transfer accepts --wait_sec S (default 6; 0 selects default; otherwise 0.6–12). Preflights IK/time through retraction, including home for vertical-transfer; no collision certification.
`robo probe-transfer --x X --y Y --z Z --to_x X --to_y Y --to_z Z [--clearance C] [--travel_z H] [--open {x,y}] [--approach {down,down45}]` compares both arms without motion/command cost. Returns routes and suggested_args for the lowest fitting motion-plus-home cost; excludes visual waits and contact/occupancy checks. Fails no_feasible_route when neither fits/reaches or home/open requirements fail. Execution IK failures return alternative_route without executing it.
After rectangle-points, in-rectangle destinations >8 mm from recorded centers fail destination_center_misaligned before planning/waiting/motion; measured_destination_check includes suggested_destination with exact measured XY and unchanged Z. Changed calibration rejects; new measurements replace old ones. Agreement does not certify physical accuracy or track displacement.
`robo finish-transfer {left,right} [--clearance C] [--wait_sec S]` retracts, homes and resumes visual waiting; requires earlier reference, open gripper and other arm home. Already-home calls only hold. Defaults C=0.03 (0.03–0.15), S=6 (0.6–12); waiting consumes action steps.
First transfer captures calibrated head depth/RGB above max(Z,to_Z)+0.06. Visual departure then 0.6 s stable return is required; RGB uses blurred coherent differences, initially absent RGB uses 8 mm depth comparison, lost captured RGB fails. Timeouts retain departure/stable_steps only across unchanged-time/reference continuations; intervening action, changed view or invalid data resets stability.
After waiting, coherent local RGB change within 25 mm of destination fails destination_changed before pickup; initially absent RGB uses >5 mm new depth relief on >=10% (minimum five) rays. Missing current data rejects; insufficient initial coverage is inconclusive. For down, symmetric source relief >6 mm off-center fails source_center_misaligned with suggested_source; ambiguous/angled relief is inconclusive. Four agreeing depth sectors reject either endpoint >12 mm below their lowest median as contact_below_regional_surface; local relief also rejects low contact.
After travel >9 cm, retained relief corroborated by unchanged local RGB, or an independently unchanged enclosed RGB feature, fails source_material_remains before lowering, leaving the gripper closed. Inspection projects a 40 mm disk onto median local surface depth; poor geometry/cropped/blank/changed imagery is inconclusive. Initially absent RGB permits depth-only comparison; lost captured RGB/calibration changes fail. Appearance cannot certify grasping; holding_verified=false.
An upward entry miss of 8–40 mm, lateral error <=8 mm and angular error <=5 degrees may return entry_retry with permitted Z bounds. One caller-corrected transfer with unchanged XY/orientation/poses/reference and no intervening action resumes from the stop, freshly preflights, raises and recenters before closing; it reuses the pre-entry destination comparison (reused_before_entry=true). The interval does not certify contact; returns resumed_entry.
Returns plan_ok, plan_fail_reason, stages, preflight, released, completion, return_pending, visual_start, visual_return, source_view, pickup_check, destination_check, measured_destination_check, alignment_check, contact_floor_check, alternative_route, entry_retry and holding_verified. Invalid arguments/data, motion errors, timeouts and episode end fail; probe/finish return their applicable subset.
