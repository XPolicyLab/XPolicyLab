`robo {vertical-transfer,deposit-transfer} {left,right} --x X --y Y --z Z --to_x X --to_y Y --to_z Z [--clearance C] [--travel_z H] [--open {x,y}] [--approach {down,down45}]`
Transfers between absolute world TCP coordinates (meters); requires open gripper and both arms home. Z is fingertip-center height; contact coordinates depend on approach orientation.
Both descend vertically, close, lift vertically, travel horizontally, lower, open, and retract vertically; deposit-transfer ends there, with home return and visual synchronization pending.
vertical-transfer additionally returns home and awaits visual departure/return; accepts --wait_sec S (default 6, range 0.6–12; 0 selects default).
Travel height defaults to max(Z,to_Z)+clearance (default 0.03); clearance range 0.03–0.15; travel_z overrides it with minimum 0.03 above both endpoints.
Preflights IK/time through retraction for deposit-transfer, including home for vertical-transfer; approach defaults to down, or explicit down45, constant from pickup through release; never substitutes orientation. No collision checking.
First transfer saves calibrated head depth; elevated rays above max(Z,to_Z)+0.06 enforce pending visual departure/return before motion. After waiting, new relief over 5 mm on >=10% (minimum five) reference rays within 25 mm of destination XY fails destination_changed before pickup; missing current depth also rejects, insufficient initial depth is inconclusive.
`robo finish-transfer {left,right} [--clearance C] [--wait_sec S]` retracts by C (default 0.03, range 0.03–0.15), returns home, then awaits visual departure/return.
finish-transfer requires an earlier transfer reference, open gripper, other arm home; already-home calls only hold. wait_sec defaults to 6, range 0.6–12; return must match within 8 mm for 0.6 s.
Returns plan_ok, plan_fail_reason, preflight, visual_return; transfers also return stages, released, completion, return_pending, visual_start, source_view, pickup_check, destination_check, alignment_check, holding_verified=false.
For down, compact symmetric depth relief over 6 mm off-center fails source_center_misaligned before motion and returns alignment_check.suggested_source (XYZ); ambiguous or angled contact is inconclusive. Local depth rejects contact below visible relief; retained relief after travel over 9 cm fails source_material_remains before lowering, leaving gripper closed at travel height. Ambiguous depth cannot certify a grasp. Also fails on bad inputs/data, motion error, timeout, or episode end; holds cost time.
