`robo surface --u U --v V [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--shape patch|plane|sphere|point] [--arm left|right] [--hue 4] [--window 100]`
Free RGB-D measurement; defaults head/patch/left. Returns center_world, offset_local, radius and fit diagnostics as applicable; rejects unavailable, ambiguous or unsupported geometry. Hue 1..30; window 3..500 pixels.
`robo grasp_span ARM --u1 U --v1 V --u2 P --v2 Q --tip_u S --tip_v T [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--inset 0.003] [--clearance 0.035] [--lift 0.045]`
Grasps a narrow horizontal span midpoint; S,T selects its connected colored sphere. Rejects broad/disconnected/unknown depth support before motion. Returns stages, span_support, baseline_stage and grasp_verified from observed lift coupling; failure may leave grip/pose changed. Inset 0..0.01, clearance 0.02..0.10, lift 0.03..0.10.
`robo tap_surface ARM --u U --v V --tip_u P --tip_v Q [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--tip_camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--clearance 0.025] [--penetration 0.004]`
Measures a colored patch and held sphere, approaches, visually recalibrates, descends and retracts. camera defaults head; tip_camera defaults camera; sphere pixels belong to tip_camera. May select another supported interior patch and perform bounded clearance, orientation, visibility or contact recovery motions. Returns stages, surface_measurement, contact_geometry and failure diagnostics; rejects invalid geometry, reach, tracking or endpoint checks. Recovery consumes action steps; retreat can fail.
`robo tap_point ARM --x X --y Y --z Z --ox A --oy B --oz C [--radius 0] [--clearance 0.025] [--penetration 0.002]`
Moves a feature with TCP-local offset A,B,C to world surface X,Y,Z with radius compensation, then retracts; preserves orientation and grip. Input geometry is not visually validated; returns stages and target_world or motion failure.
ARM is left|right; distances are meters and image coordinates are pixels. Contact commands preserve grip; clearance 0.01..0.15, penetration 0..0.005, radius 0..0.10. Motion commands consume action steps; surface does not.
All commands return plan_ok/plan_fail_reason; malformed input or unavailable observations fail. Contact results retain contact_verified=false: accepted geometry or constrained motion does not verify physical contact.
