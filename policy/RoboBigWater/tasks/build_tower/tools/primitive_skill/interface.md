`robo skill_prepare {left,right} --source_u U --source_v V --target_u U --target_v V --floor Z [--clearance 0.015] [--preferred_yaw 0] [--place_yaw DEG] [--grasp_axis DEG] [--via_x X --via_y Y --via_z Z]` derives the current transfer recipe, optionally inserts one loaded via waypoint, preflights all public-model paths and action budget, and returns a single-use lift ticket; free, no motion.
Checks exactly two equivalent empty-finger signs, reversing the source and release orientation together so payload rotation is unchanged. Returns all candidate_checks and chooses the valid lower planned-step bound. No sign switching occurs after grasp or during a failed phase.
`robo skill_execute {left,right} --ticket TOKEN` consumes one phase ticket. Lift executes approach/grasp/lift and requires observed surface translation; success returns next_ticket. Place executes carry/lower/release/retract and requires distributed observed surface correspondence.
Uses existing timed joint-motion and gripper primitives: empty approach solves endpoint IK and follows the native joint-target path; contact, lift, carry, lower and retract use Cartesian lines. Timing constants are unchanged. All physical steps count against the episode. Prepare-to-lift binds full public depth/calibration and both robot states. After a verified lift, the place handoff binds calibration, the inactive arm and gripper states while allowing held-object depth and active-arm settle; place re-plans from current active-arm joints.
Stops before gripper closure/release when pose error exceeds3mm/3degrees, after any execution failure, episode end or unverified surface effect. A failed phase never returns an automatic retry ticket. A closed command may remain closed; inspect the public result before recovery.
Preflight reserves ten possible settle steps per movement and eight per gripper command, and checks inactive TCP separation>=0.18m. Public planning and surface matching do not certify full collision clearance, semantic identity, stability or official completion. Refresh geometry after failure; invoke the ordinary home only after visible release/detachment.
