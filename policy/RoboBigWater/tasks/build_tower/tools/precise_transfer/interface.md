`robo surface --u U --v V [--tolerance 0.002]` measures a connected horizontal face; `robo surfaces [--tolerance 0.002] [--format compact|full]` inventories faces with calibrated normals within 10 degrees of vertical. Free observations; tolerance 0.0005–0.005 m. Invalid depth, arguments, absent or ambiguous geometry return plan_ok=false and plan_fail_reason.
For an unambiguous initial scene, surfaces first returns initial_assembly: an eight-part layered proposal recomputed from current shapes/colors, with future support heights explicitly marked as predictions. Ambiguous/missing roles return available=false; layer_plan accepts selected current pixels. This does not certify manipulation or completion. Measurements include world top_center, visible boundary-rectangle dimensions/direction, bounds, samples, surrounding_plane_z, height_above_surroundings_m and nearby_lower_levels (up to four [world Z, sample count] modes, at least 40 samples each). Lower geometry is sampled within a 0.05 m expanded footprint, 0.004–0.20 m below; unknown values are null, not inferred thickness/contact. Inventory adds pixels, counts and height_bands; compact face_columns/face_rows round to 4 decimals, full returns unrounded dictionaries. Chromatic_scene adds up to eight color-diverse RGB-depth regions from the same observation with omission counts; RGB failure preserves depth results. Occlusion, touching colors and slopes can hide, merge or split regions; centers are not certified grasp poses.
`robo transfer {left,right} --x X --y Y --z Z --to_x X --to_y Y --to_z Z [--open x|y] [--finger_sign auto|positive|negative] [--yaw 0] [--yaw_symmetry exact|half_turn] [--grasp_yaw 0] [--clearance 0.10] [--peer_clearance 0.18] [--motion separate|compact] [--entry vertical|diagonal] [--departure vertical|diagonal] [--landing vertical|diagonal] [--lift_mode full|rising] [--lift_x X --lift_y Y [--lift_z Z]] [--retreat vertical|diagonal] [--park start|source|none] [--park_x X --park_y Y]` grasps, transports, releases and retreats; internal motions consume action steps.
Source/destination XYZ are grasp/release TCP positions in world meters, not support heights. With known support levels, release Z = source Z + destination support Z - source support Z + 0.002 m. Defaults: open=x, finger_sign=auto, yaw_symmetry=exact, motion=separate, lift_mode=full, park=start; entry/departure/landing/retreat=vertical. Clearance 0.015–0.25 m; the 15 mm lower bound is the calibrated URAI migration candidate, not a collision guarantee. Transport height H=max(source Z, release Z)+clearance. Empty approach uses source Z+clearance; full loaded lift reaches H before translation. Every payload/finger/wrist sweep requires caller-certified clearance.
Vertical entry hovers then descends; compact combines the elevated empty turn/travel, retaining max(entry Z, source Z+clearance) during turns. An already aligned/open elevated TCP within 0.001 m of source XY can descend without a hover stop. Diagonal entry finishes orientation above contact, opens, then descends with fixed orientation; if horizontal distance exceeds clearance, elevated travel precedes a final diagonal segment starting one clearance from source XY.
Paired lift_x/lift_y specify a waypoint after vertical detachment by clearance; lift_z requires the pair and lies from source Z+clearance to H (default H). Diagonal departure requires the pair and goes directly from grasp to waypoint with fixed orientation. Rising applies only to compact without a waypoint: vertical landing uses source-clearance lift then rising transport; diagonal landing to a destination above source-clearance height rises to H at an intermediate XY before descending. Other diagonal routes retain full lift. Vertical landing transports at H then lowers; diagonal landing descends directly from lift/waypoint, except the stated rising case. Oblique contact paths have no automatic vertical fallback.
Grasp_yaw is a world-Z offset in [-180,180] degrees applied to the chosen opening axis before approach (default 0); finger_sign selects the sign before this offset. Supply it from current public geometry and clear the rotated approach sweep. Yaw is a separate relative world-Z rotation in [-180,180] degrees after lift/at waypoint; compact combines yaw with transport. Auto finger_sign may try an equivalent opposite empty orientation; explicit signs forbid that alternative. Half_turn explicitly permits +/-180-degree equivalent payload yaw and certifies both sweeps. Bounded combined/separate and symmetry alternatives require stationary, unclipped IK rejection and an active episode; executed turns followed by failed translation stop closed. Returns executed_yaw.
Start parking returns to entry XY at max(entry Z,H); paired park_x/park_y replaces this XY only for park=start. Source parking returns to source XY at H; none ends above release. Vertical retreat precedes return; compact start parking combines remaining rise with return after reaching H. Diagonal retreat requires start/source parking and first rises vertically by clearance, then travels to the same parking endpoint with fixed release orientation. Opening does not prove physical separation; return sweeps require clearance.
Peer_clearance 0.05–0.30 m rejects checked TCP route segments too near the other TCP before motion; this is not full collision checking. Returns plan_ok, plan_fail_reason, stages, reached_tcp, closure_requested, release_requested, grasp_verified=false and placement_verified=false. Stops on planning failure, clipping, inaccurate motion or episode end; failure after closure can leave the command closed. No regrasp retries; request flags do not verify contact.
Success includes a free post_release_scene before stages: scene_overview of up to eight color-diverse regions plus face_columns/face_rows of up to eight horizontal faces ordered by release relevance. Columns identify pixels, centers, dimensions, headings, lower levels/gaps, signed center-minus-release offsets, inventory indices and overlap flags; counts disclose omissions. Overview retains median_rgb, visible_center, height_range_m, upper_band_center and upper-band heading/span. Offsets are not tracked payload errors. Detailed fits remain available through surfaces/regions; observation failure returns available=false without changing completed motion success.
Headings are observed XY directions counterclockwise from world +X modulo 180 degrees in [-90,90), not wrist yaw. Horizontal long_heading_deg is null at visible aspect ratio <=1.2. Upper-band center uses at least five samples within 0.003 m of the 99th-percentile height; contour heading/span require eight samples, covariance ratio >3 and 5th–95th percentile span >=0.006 m, otherwise null. Partial faces/ridges can bias geometry; no measurement certifies identity, stability or completion.
