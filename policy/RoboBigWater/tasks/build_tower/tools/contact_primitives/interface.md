`robo push|pull|drag {left,right} --x X --y Y --z Z --to_x X --to_y Y [--open x|y] [--approach 0.04] [--clearance 0.025] [--grip 0]` composes native approach, open, contact, grip, planar motion, release and withdrawal. `pull` reverses the measured XY endpoints; `drag` keeps the grip command during the planar motion.
All coordinates are current public world metres. Every motion consumes budget; bounded approach is 5–150mm, withdrawal clearance 10–150mm and displacement must exceed5mm. Refuses invalid/nonfinite inputs and stops on any native planning/tracking/episode failure.
Returns stages plus an attempted local public-depth registration in the source/target height band. `effect_verified=true` requires at least60% matched points,20 points and6 XY cells after the requested displacement; otherwise `effect_verified=false` is retained. No command completion proves contact or material stability. Do not retry an uncertain dispatch or replay coordinates in another episode.
