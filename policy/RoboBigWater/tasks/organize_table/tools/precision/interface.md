All distances are metres in world coordinates; XYZ denotes TCP goals. Motion syntax uses positional `ARM=left|right`, required `--x X --y Y --z Z`, optional `--clearance M` (default 0.05, range 0.01–0.25) and `--tolerance M` (default 0.01, range 0.003–0.025). Optional arguments below show defaults; each value follows its named flag.
`robo surface_box <head|wrist_l|wrist_r> --u0 U --v0 V --u1 U --v1 V [--zmin -10 --zmax 10 --ymin -10 --ymax 10 --seed_u -1 --seed_v -1 --link 0.008 --depth_span 0.04 --push_dx 0 --push_dy 0]`: free RGB-D rectangle measurement; returns median, percentile bounds, extent, count, height/Y profiles and footprint yaw. Paired seed pixels select connected depth (link 0.001–0.03, depth_span 0.005–0.20); seed output includes selected_fraction and touches_roi_edge. A nonzero push vector of length 0.01–0.25 returns visible convex-area center and centerline contact/exit XY, not TCP goals. Invalid bounds/seeds, missing depth or fewer than nine samples fail; cropping, occlusion and mixed geometry limit interpretation.
`robo checked_pick ARM --x X --y Y --z Z [--open x|y --approach down|down45 --clearance M --lift 0.05 --tolerance M --verify_radius 0.04]`: raises before rotation as needed, travels above entry, opens, enters, closes and withdraws; defaults open=x, approach=down. Lift range 0.02–0.20 has effective minimum 0.08; down45 entry/withdrawal follows (0,+1,-1)/(0,-1,+1). Observed upper depth may increase entry clearance. Returns source_z, entry_tcp, transit_z, requested/effective lift, source_check and lift_check; visibly empty source or negative/unconfirmed lift evidence can stop with no release.
`robo checked_place ARM --x X --y Y --z Z [--clearance M --tolerance M --transit_z 0 --retract 0 --entry auto|vertical --carry_clearance 0.08 --source_z Z]`: requires commanded closure, preserves orientation, transports, enters, opens and retreats. Auto entry follows TCP +X when within 60 degrees of downward; otherwise vertical. Departure is at least source_z + max(carry_clearance,0.08), never below current TCP; source_z defaults to current TCP Z and cannot exceed it by more than tolerance. Carry clearance range 0–0.25; transit_z=0 is automatic, explicit height must reach goal Z + clearance and cannot bypass departure. Retract=0 means max(clearance,0.08); explicit 0.01–0.25 also has a 0.08 floor. Returns effective waypoints, departure_credit_m, carry_checks and released; negative carry evidence stops closed before release, unavailable evidence continues.
`robo checked_transfer ARM --x X --y Y --z Z --to_x X --to_y Y --to_z Z [--open x|y --approach down|down45 --clearance M --lift 0.05 --tolerance M --verify_radius 0.04 --transit_z 0 --allow_unverified 0|1]`: composes pick/place, credits completed lift and uses axial placement with >=0.08 retreat. Requires positive translated-surface evidence by default; allow_unverified=1 admits inconclusive evidence but not detected misses or sufficiently visible empty predictions. Returns phase, nested pick/place feedback, phase-tagged stages and released; a failed phase stops the sequence without early release.
`robo checked_pull ARM --x X --y Y --z Z [--standoff 0.06 --distance 0.15 --inset 0.01 --open x|z --clearance M --tolerance M --verify_radius 0.025 --allow_unverified 0|1]`: approaches along +Y, seats at Y+inset, closes and pulls along -Y, without release. Defaults open=z, allow_unverified=0; standoff 0.02–0.20, distance 0.02–0.25, inset 0–0.02. Requires a depth reference before motion and positive displacement evidence; with a reference, distance >=0.08 probes after 0.05 travel. Override permits unavailable/inconclusive evidence but never not_pulled. Returns contact_tcp, pull_check, pull_probe_check when applicable and measured displacement; failed probe stops closed.
`robo checked_push ARM --x X --y Y --z Z --dx DX --dy DY [--standoff 0.025 --clearance M --retract 0.08 --tolerance M]`: closed-hand horizontal stroke, DX/DY measured from contact XYZ. Raises >=0.08 before rotation, aims 45 degrees down along travel, enters axially behind contact, strokes, disengages axially up/back by up to 0.03 vertical rise and retreats to at least transit height. Vector length 0.01–0.25, standoff 0.01–0.10, retract 0.02–0.25. Returns push_from_tcp, push_to_tcp, entry/disengage/retreat goals and contact_verified=false, displacement_verified=false; no retries or release.
Verification radius is 0 (disabled) or 0.015–0.08. Source probes and lift evidence combine calibrated head/wrist views; missing/occluded samples add no empty vote. Positive lift evidence requires >=20 translated matches and >=70% of decisive samples. Transfer override still rejects >=20 empty predictions exceeding half the reference or >=70% of empty-plus-matched votes. Carry checks use a forward/lower patch, try each view independently and refresh after source ascent. These checks can reject uncertain motion and cannot prove identity, retention or placement; grasp_verified remains false.
All commands return plan_ok/plan_fail_reason; motion returns stages and reached TCP. Invalid arguments/waypoints fail; planner failure, clipping, exhaustion, position error above tolerance or angle error above 8 degrees stop execution except bounded guarded recoveries. Pick may reverse a blocked descent or reorder orientation/XY travel; place may try up to two XY corner orders; pull may rotate before retrying rejected transit. IK recovery requires unchanged measured pose and command-specific guards; no blind contact retry. Every motion costs action steps, paths are not collision-checked, and released=true persists if retreat fails after opening.
