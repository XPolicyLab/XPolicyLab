`robo surface --u U --v V [--camera C]` returns live-depth `surface_world` in metres, without motion. All pixels use full resolution, top-left origin; C=head (default)|wrist_l|wrist_r.
`robo grasp-fit --u U --v V [--support Z] [--camera C]` is free; returns support_z/source, sample_count, span_m, footprint_center_xy, fit_mode/count and suggested_grasp (pixel, yaw, opening, support). Estimates omitted support; explicit disagreement >.004 m fails. Fits connected material .004–.15 m above support; unsupported centers use a local seed patch. Fails on missing/ambiguous support, bad depth, sparse/clipped/oversized regions or unusable width; touching regions and hidden shape remain unresolved.
`robo pixel-grasp left|right --u U --v V [--camera C] [--yaw DEG] [--tilt DEG] [--bearing DEG] [--orientation tilted|current] [--opening F] [--inset M] [--support Z] [--clearance M] [--lift M] [--u2 U2 --v2 V2]` approaches with initial rotation, orients at hover, descends, closes and lifts. Paired edge pixels override center/yaw/opening; require tilted mode, tilt=0, span .002–.076 m and edge-height difference ≤.015 m.
`robo pixel-place left|right --u U --v V [--camera C] [--yaw DEG] [--tilt DEG] [--bearing DEG] [--orientation tilted|current] [--height M] [--clearance M] [--plane Z]` raises with initial rotation, combines travel/rotation, lowers and opens at the reached pose. Plane projects the pixel ray onto world-z Z; otherwise uses live depth. Returns surface_source and place_path; plane accuracy is unverified.
`robo pixel-transfer left|right --u U --v V --du DU --dv DV [--camera C] [--yaw DEG] [--tilt DEG] [--release_tilt DEG] [--bearing DEG] [--opening F] [--support Z] [--inset M] [--lift M] [--height M] [--plane Z] [--clearance M] [--release_path auto|raised] [--travel_z Z]` performs guarded grasp/transfer/release; returns nested grasp/place and transfer_stage=grasp|place|complete. Plane destination is frozen before motion. Applies opening before combined approach/rotation; raises low starting poses first.
Angles are degrees: yaw=-180..180 (default 0) sets opening axis from world +x toward +y before tilt; bearing=-180..180 (default 90) sets tilt direction likewise. Tilt=0..60 defaults to grasp 0, placement 45; transfer release_tilt defaults to 45 independently. Standalone orientation=current preserves initial wrist rotation and ignores validated angles; default tilted returns approach_dir.
Opening=.1..1 (default .65) of .088 m gap; inset=-.025...025 m (default .008) targets below surface. Support overrides inset, targeting halfway to the surface, which must be .004–.15 m above support. Height=.02...25 m (default .10) adds world-z release offset; clearance=.04...20 (default .08), lift=.04...20 (default .10). Support accuracy/contact are unverified. Grasp hover is max(target z + clearance, surface z + .04).
With plane and no travel_z, transfer estimates persistent corridor geometry from frozen depth before approach, at hover and after descent; radius=opening*.088/2+.015 m. Cross-view exclusion requires a fully valid 3x3 neighborhood >.015 m behind a point; occluded/invalid/out-of-view points persist. Returns initial/observed_corridor_z, estimated_load_below_tcp_m and route_clearance_z (corridor + clearance + estimated load). Auto uses direct release if plane+height clears that floor; otherwise raises to at least max(floor, plane+height+clearance). Raised forces the raised route; requested lift remains a minimum. Persistent robot geometry may inflate clearance.
Travel_z requires plane and bypasses corridor inference; returns route_observation=caller_supplied, requested_travel_z and route_verified=false. Must be finite and ≥max(source surface+clearance+estimated load below TCP, plane+height); raised also requires plane+height+clearance. Invalid heights fail before motion. Auto descends after transit unless transit equals release height; actual transit is place.travel_z. Intermediate clearance and full/tilted load extent remain unverified.
Stationary preflight IK failures permit one alternative: standalone grasp combines travel/rotation; standalone place separates them; transfer approach separates them; transfer release tries 60° tilt along source-to-destination XY. No such retry after executed failure or in current mode. Transfer alone permits one bounded near-contact close/lift recovery after descent error. Returns per-stage errors, selected paths and fallback angles.
All commands return plan_ok/plan_fail_reason; invalid inputs fail without motion. Motion stops on planner failure, >.01 m position error, >8° orientation error or episode end, except bounded descent recovery above; no release after failed transfer motion. Grasp source_evidence=cleared|surface_remains|occluded|unknown, grasp_verified=false; release reports released and placement_verified=false. Motion success does not prove retention, containment or final placement.
