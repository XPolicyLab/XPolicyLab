## axis_grasp
`robo axis_grasp ARM --x X --y Y --z Z [--mode vertical|horizontal] [--ax X --ay Y] [--clearance M] [--lift M] [--path direct|staged] [--reorient combined|separate] [--transit raised|direct] [--idle_clearance auto|off] [--carry_orientation auto|grasp|forward] [--release_x X --release_y Y --release_z Z]`
Opens, approaches a world-meter center, inserts, closes, extracts and optionally releases; one command plus motion steps. ARM is left or right; mode defaults to vertical.
Vertical entry is +Y/X opening, or 30-degree side entry for rearward, predominantly lateral travel; horizontal entry is -Z with opening perpendicular to nonzero XY axis (ax=1, ay=0). Clearance defaults to 0.04 m (0.04–0.25); lift to 0.10 m (0.02–0.25).
Reorient defaults to combined rotation during approach; separate rotates first. Combined approach permits at most six roll/inclination/side-entry plans after IK rejections with unchanged time and both TCP poses. Approach pose error ≤30 mm/5 degrees permits one 40 mm standoff recovery only for original clearance >40 mm.
Transit defaults to raised for long upright arrivals: elevated standoff at max(initial Z,Z+lift). Transit=direct still retains elevation for sideways travel >2×clearance. Path defaults to direct, combining raised descent/insertion; staged separates them. Matching forward wrists may defer rearward side-entry yaw to descent, with one guarded entry-orientation transit alternative.
Idle clearance defaults to auto: check initial arrival polyline within 0.32 m of idle TCP; off checks only final insertion. A nearby idle gripper <0.99 open stops; otherwise one outward X retreat beyond checked X extent by 0.328 m and 0.12 m rise preserves rotation. Residual proximity, retreat failure or active TCP drift stops; this is not full collision checking.
Optional release XYZ requires all three finite coordinates, release Z≥Z+lift, and a reachable endpoint. Without release XYZ, lift vertically and retain grip. Vertical delivery extracts vertically; horizontal rotating direct delivery extracts diagonally to lift height (at most half the lateral distance), with a guarded vertical alternative; staged extracts vertically.
Carry orientation defaults to auto: horizontal carry rotates to +Y/X after extraction; upright carry preserves entry except rearward side entry/reverse-X transport normalizes yaw during lift. Grasp preserves orientation; forward selects +Y/X. Auto permits bounded IK alternatives: upright lift/carry yaw alternatives or horizontal rotation while rising another lift distance before translation. Each requires unchanged time and both TCP poses; failed transport prevents opening.
Returns plan_ok, plan_fail_reason, stages with per-motion sim_duration_s, reached TCP, released and grasp_verified=false; neither closure nor release confirms retention or landing.
Invalid inputs, clipping, motion failure, pose error >8 mm/5 degrees outside the bounded approach recovery, or exhausted time stop execution. Insertion failure stops open. Caller must provide clear swept volumes and support/rotation clearance; all routes can fail IK.
