## axis_grasp
`robo axis_grasp ARM --x X --y Y --z Z [--mode vertical|horizontal] [--ax X --ay Y] [--clearance M] [--lift M] [--path direct|staged] [--reorient combined|separate] [--transit raised|direct] [--idle_clearance auto|off] [--carry_orientation auto|grasp|forward] [--release_x X --release_y Y --release_z Z]`
Opens, approaches a world-meter center, inserts, closes, extracts and optionally releases; one command plus motion steps. ARM=left|right; mode=vertical by default.
Vertical entry is +Y with X opening or geometry-selected side entry; horizontal entry is -Z with opening perpendicular to nonzero XY axis (default ax=1, ay=0). Clearance defaults to 0.04 m (0.04–0.25); lift=0.10 m (0.02–0.25).
Defaults: path=staged, reorient=combined, transit=raised, idle_clearance=auto, carry_orientation=auto. Staged entry separates standoff descent and insertion; direct combines them. Separate rotates before travel; long sideways upright arrivals retain elevated transit even with direct.
Idle clearance checks the arrival polyline against 0.32 m TCP separation and may retreat an open idle arm outward/upward; off still checks insertion. This is not full collision checking; swept-volume and support clearance are caller assumptions.
Release XYZ is all-or-none, finite, with release Z≥Z+lift. Omitted release retains grip after vertical lift. Delivery extracts vertically first; auto normalizes horizontal carry to +Y/X, grasp preserves entry orientation, forward selects +Y/X.
Bounded roll/inclination/carry alternatives require nonexecuting IK rejection with unchanged time and both TCP poses. Approach recovery may shorten a >40 mm standoff once, including pose error ≤30 mm/5 degrees. Horizontal auto delivery with lift≥0.08 m may half-lift, then rotate/rise; a rejected rotation permits one guarded rising shift toward release. Partial-height clearance is assumed.
Horizontal mode checks local head-depth support: inadequate clearance returns horizontal_center_near_support; competing planes require clearance above every candidate uncertainty or return support_height_ambiguous. Missing evidence preserves execution; no center correction is applied.
Returns plan_ok, plan_fail_reason, support_check, stages with sim_duration_s, reached TCP, released and grasp_verified=false. Invalid inputs, clipping, motion failure, time exhaustion or pose error >8 mm/5 degrees outside bounded approach recovery stop; insertion failure stops open, failed transport prevents release. Closure/release never verifies retention or landing.
