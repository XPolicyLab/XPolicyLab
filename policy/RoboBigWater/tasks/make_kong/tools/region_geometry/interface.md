`robo compare_faces --reference '[camera,u,v]' --faces '[[camera,u,v],...]' [--count N --tolerance M]`: read-only comparison of planar colored fronts across head/wrist_l/wrist_r views, normalizing contrast and color to each pale background; seeds are pale face interiors as [camera,u,v] or [camera,u,v,u0,v0,u1,v1] with exclusive crop bounds; count defaults to 3, more candidates than count required. Returns selected_indices, unresolved seed roles/indices/reasons, retained per-face distances, measured surface centers/normals, background_rgb and separation; selects nothing if any front is unresolved; fails on unresolved patterns, insufficient depth or ambiguous separation or overlapping crops (merged_fronts).
`robo inspect_regions [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--u0 N --v0 N --u1 N --v1 N] [--min_pixels N] [--top_tolerance M]`
Reads fresh RGB-D without motion or command cost; defaults to the full head image and 30 pixels, with exclusive rectangle upper bounds.
Returns pale connected regions, pixel boxes, visible world bounds/midpoints, top_z, upper_patches and multiple_upper_patches. Each upper patch has center_xy, z, extent_xy, narrower_axis, pixels, pixel_box, near_horizontal and touches_roi_edge.
Upper patches are separate connected areas within top_tolerance of the region's upper height after excluding its highest 2% of samples (default 0.001 m, range 0.0005–0.004), with ≥10 pixels and ≥6 mm XY spans. top_center_xy, top_extent_xy and narrower_top_axis are null unless exactly one patch is within 15° of horizontal and clear of the ROI border. Unresolved seams can merge, colors can fragment, lower areas are omitted. Patterns: G=green, R=red, #=dark, .=pale.
`robo inspect_surface --u N --v N [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--tolerance M]`
Reads fresh depth without motion or cost; fits a plane in a 5×5 seed neighborhood and grows connected coplanar pixels regardless of color.
Tolerance defaults to 0.002 m (range 0.0005–0.005); seed must be at least two pixels from image edges, with valid depth and a planar neighborhood.
Returns surface_center, normal toward camera, seed_world, visible bounds, extent_xyz, pixel_box, pixels and touches_image_edge; world distances are meters.
Both return plan_ok/plan_fail_reason; fail on invalid inputs, absent calibration/depth or insufficient area. Surface fitting also fails on nonplanar seeds.
Visible centers are not body centers or TCP targets; occlusion can truncate bounds and touching coplanar faces can merge. Measurements expire after motion.
