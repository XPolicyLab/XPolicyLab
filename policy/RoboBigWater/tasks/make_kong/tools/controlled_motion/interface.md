`robo transfer <left|right> --x X --y Y --z Z --to_x X --to_y Y --to_z Z [options]`: grasp, lift, carry, rotate, lower, release, retract; coordinates are absolute world TCP meters, without body-offset compensation; endpoint distance ≤0.6 m.
`robo lift_grasp <left|right> --x X --y Y --z Z [pickup options]`: grasp/lift only, retain closure, return inspection_required=true. `robo place_grasp <left|right> --to_x X --to_y Y --to_z Z [placement options]`: carry/rotate/lower/release/retract from measured pose; requires commanded closure, not verified contact.
`robo check_transfer` / `robo check_place_grasp` accept the corresponding execution arguments for fresh motionless, cost-free previews. `robo scan_transfer` accepts transfer arguments and compares tilts 0/15/30/45/60°, headings 0/180/90/−90°, both wrist branches: 34 candidates; explicit azimuth gives ten, explicit wrist halves either count. Returns reproducible arguments, feasibility, waypoints and costs.
`robo push_line` accepts transfer arguments: close above source, descend, stroke to destination, retract; rejects nondefault aperture controls and nonzero place_pitch; omits depth guards. All execution costs action steps; previews do not move.
Pickup options: --open x|y (y), --wrist nearest|opposite (nearest; scan tries both), --aperture F (1; 0<F≤1), --approach down|down45 (down), --tilt DEG (0–60, overrides approach), --azimuth DEG (−180..180; default 0=+y, 90=+x, 180=−y, −90=−x). Opening projects perpendicular to approach; opposite turns 180° about approach. Shared options: --clearance M (0.065; 0.025–0.2), --entry axis|vertical (axis), --peer_margin M (0.10; 0–0.3), --hand_margin M (0.012; 0–0.04).
Placement options: --release_aperture F (inherits aperture for transfer; 1 for place_grasp), --place_pitch DEG (0; −90..90), --place_axis grip|world_x|world_y|world_z (grip), --withdrawal axis|entry (axis), --carry_margin M (0.065; 0–0.15), --destination_margin M (0.025; 0–0.1), --release_halfspan M (0.06; 0–0.12). Rotation follows right-hand rule; grip-axis world sign changes between wrist branches. Held placement retains measured pickup orientation and accepts no pickup options.
Additional pickup options: --source_radius M (0.008; 0–0.04), --source_below M (0.008; 0–0.03), --pickup_halfspan M (0.06; 0–0.12). Source evidence requires ≥3 depth samples within radius in XY and Z−source_below..Z+0.03; after lift, ≥80% and ≥3 predicted samples with fully observed empty 25 mm displacement envelopes cause pickup_not_retained. Missing lift depth fails; occlusion is inconclusive; previews omit the lift check. Returns source_depth/lift_depth; no holding guarantee.
Fresh head-depth guards reject ≥3 obstructing samples: pickup rails at ±pickup_halfspan along opening, 0–40 mm behind TCP, radius 6 mm, swept through descent; hand axis 40–100 mm behind TCP swept through insertion with hand_margin; destination lowering capsule with destination_margin; release rectangle ±release_halfspan along opening and 0–40 mm behind final TCP, thickness radius 8 mm. Return pickup_depth/hand_depth/destination_depth/release_depth and occupied/unavailable reasons. Rails/rectangle are independent of aperture, exclude hidden geometry and are uncalibrated proxies; zero radius/halfspan disables its guard, source_radius=0 also disables lift evidence.
Carry depth selects lowest cruise ≥ original height with fewer than three corridor samples within ±(carry_margin+0.005 m) vertically; allows distant overhead surfaces, rechecks rises, fails on missing depth or extra rise >0.2 m. Added cruise rise/return segments are checked with radius carry_margin+0.005 m; carry_transition_occupied rejects ≥3 obstructing samples before motion. Returns carry_depth with transition endpoints and samples; carry_margin=0 disables. Peer guard rejects path segments nearer than peer_margin to other measured TCP; peer_margin=0 disables; returns peer_clearance. Neither check models full bodies.
Transit is ≥65 mm above contact heights with horizontal arrival before descent and opening before ingress. Entry selects axial/vertical insertion; withdrawal=axis follows final approach, withdrawal=entry follows entry. Axis placement requires ≥15° below horizontal and staging offset ≤0.35 m. Near-horizontal axial withdrawal travels max(clearance,0.065 m) then rises; upward exits or offsets >0.35 m fail. Preflight chooses cheapest reachable destination/combined/departure rotation, preferring existing alternatives on ties; sweeps remain unchecked.
Returns plan_ok/plan_fail_reason, waypoints, stage errors, reached_tcp, clearance_rotation and costs excluding home travel; does not home. Invalid arguments, absent required depth, blocked guards, unreachable paths, insufficient budget or measured TCP error >8 mm / 6° stop; no retry or release after failure. Release requires requested and measured opening ≥0.03 above measured grasp opening; release_not_clear stops before retraction without automatic widening. Accurate tracking does not verify grasp, clearance or placement.
