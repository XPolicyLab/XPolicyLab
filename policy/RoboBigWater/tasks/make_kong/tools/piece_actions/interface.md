`robo tip_pieces <left|right> --pieces '[[x,y,top_z],...]' --height H --thickness T [--face_y -1|1 --clearance M --min_clearance M]` makes separate upper-edge pushes and reopens; check_tip_pieces previews without motion.
`robo relay_stand <left|right> --x X --y Y --z Z --middle_x X --middle_y Y --middle_z Z --to_x X --to_y Y --to_z Z --height H --thickness T` carries through a resting point using both arms and turns a flat piece upright; check_relay_stand previews.
`tip_then_relay` / `check_tip_then_relay` accept relay arguments plus --tip JSON containing arm, pieces, height, thickness and optional face_y/clearance/min_clearance; they prepend pushes to the carry.
Coordinates are measured world body centers in metres, except upper centers for pushes; each relay leg ≤0.6 m. H>T, H 0.025–0.12, T 0.005–0.05.
Relay options: --flat_axis x|y (y), --offset_z M (0; ±T/2), --clearance M (0.10; 0.045–0.2), --face_y -1|1 (−1), --aperture F (0.65), --source_aperture F (1), --release_aperture F (0.6), --carry_guard 0|1 (1); openings range 0.1–1. For flat_axis=x the directed long axis +x turns to +z; the donor points down and opens along x; the receiver opens along y and finishes pointing −x/down.
Push clearance defaults to 0.045, minimum 0.025; raised-waypoint reachability can reduce it to the minimum. Relay search previews wrist alternatives and rotation locations before motion.
Returns plan_ok/plan_fail_reason, waypoints, selected_relay, costs without home travel and stage errors. Depth, reachability, budget, peer distance, opening and tracking checks can stop execution without retry. For x-axis carries, donor clearance reverses the flat transit without wrist rotation. Resting-face depth is checked after donor clearance and before receiver approach; post-lift depth checks retention. The x-axis carry includes eight settling steps after receiving closure, included in preview cost and returned as receive_settle_steps. Redundant opening targets are omitted; both hands finish at least 60% open. Preview is free; every motion consumes action steps; physical completion is unverified.
