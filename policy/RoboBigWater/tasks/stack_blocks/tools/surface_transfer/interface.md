### surface_transfer
`robo surface_transfer ARM --source_u U --source_v V --target_u X --target_v Y --ref_u R --ref_v S [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--inset D] [--clearance C] [--park home|both|ready|retreat] [--place_route compact|high] [--radius M] [--tolerance T] [--yaw auto|preserve] [--grasp auto|down|forward]`
Measures both surfaces from one calibrated-depth observation, then grasps, lifts, transfers, releases and parks. ARM=left|right must start open; motion consumes simulation time.
Pixels are integer column/row interiors of horizontal surfaces; R/S selects the source bottom plane, below both tops. Uses visible connected-surface centres; occlusion or touching coplanar surfaces can bias geometry.
Release TCP Z=destination top+source height−D+0.002 m; assumes upright centred retention. D=0.01 m (0<D≤0.04 and D<height≤0.30); C=0.03 m (0.03–0.20); lift clears both measured tops by C, capped at 0.30 m.
Camera=head, M=0.08 m (0.01–0.30), T=0.002 m (0.0005–0.005). Inputs must be finite. Place_route=compact transfers diagonally to release; high retains altitude then descends.
Grasp=auto directs tilt toward the destination for surfaces straddling the live TCP bisector toward the other arm, or an outward destination farther than the source and beyond half the inter-TCP XY distance. Tilt=45° beyond 75% of the own-to-other span, otherwise 22.5°; one alternate tilt follows wholly motion-free approach IK rejection. Otherwise pickup is downward with bounded yaw/tilt recovery. Forward forces destination-directed 45°; down prohibits tilt. Selection is heuristic, not a feasibility test.
Grasp orientation is initially preserved. Yaw=auto permits bounded +90/−90° world-Z placement retries after motion-free IK failures, changing final item yaw; preserve disables them.
Park=home returns the selected arm after opening; both returns both concurrently and requires the other gripper already commanded open. Compact home/both returns directly; high first retreats vertically. Ready retreats vertically then 0.12 m toward the incoming side without wrist rotation; retreat stays overhead. Paths/returns are not collision-checked; vertical/inter-arm clearance is not guaranteed.
Returns plan_ok/plan_fail_reason, plan_detail, source/target geometry, lift_distance, grasp_mode, destination_span_fraction for automatic directed grasps, pickup_attempts, nested pickup/placement feedback, released and unverified placement status.
Fails before motion on invalid arguments/geometry or overlapping footprints; stops on planning, clipping, tracking failures or episode end without restarting. Partial motion or release may have occurred; execution does not verify retention or stability.
