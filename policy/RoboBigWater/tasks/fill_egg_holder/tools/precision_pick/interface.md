`robo round_center --u U --v V [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--pixels 8]`: motion-free convex depth fit; returns world center, radius, RMS and samples; rejects invalid/nonconvex fits or RMS >1 mm.
`robo release_clearance --x X --y Y --z Z [--radius 0.05]`: motion-free head-depth column check; returns ceiling, coverage and nearby candidates; rejects missing depth or surfaces >30 mm above Z. Radius range .03–.10 m.
`robo checked_pick ARM --x X --y Y --z Z [--open x|y] [--aperture 0.75] [--offset -0.01] [--clearance 0.09] [--lift 0.09]`: downward grasp and vertical lift; clearance/lift .04–.20 m, offset -.01–.03 m; local depth/load extent bounds approach and exit height.
`robo checked_transfer ARM --x X --y Y --z Z --to_x X --to_y Y --to_z Z [--open x|y] [--aperture 0.75] [--offset -0.01] [--margin 0.05] [--radius 0.05] [--segment 0.20]`: grasp, lift, depth-covered transit, vertical release, opening and retreat; requires commanded open gripper.
ARM=left|right; positions/lengths are absolute world coordinates/metres; source XYZ is a center and destination XYZ a nominal TCP release point. Open defaults to x; aperture .5–1; transfer offset -.01–0, margin .03–.12, radius .03–.10, segment .03–.20.
Both grasp commands require a fresh source fit within 12 mm, preshape overhead and fully close; missing source or exclusive positive lift evidence fails. A qualified near-TCP depth obstruction permits at most one open-arm view relocation and fresh checks; no grasp retries or rollback.
Transfer checks the release column, plans covered capsules with bounded detours and expands clearance for observed load extent. Excessive height, missing depth, out-of-bounds calibration or envelope fails; release Z is max(depth floor, grasp calibration), raised at most 30 mm above request; XY stays fixed.
Every loaded height change precedes horizontal travel; downward transport and release portions are ≤30 mm. Retention is checked after each loaded segment and before opening using a fixed post-lift anchor; absent/ambiguous evidence returns transport_not_visually_confirmed.
Motion stops on planning failure, clipping, episode end or TCP error >8 mm / 6 degrees; grasp-only shallow contact allows ≤15 mm total/upward and ≤10 mm XY error under source-height checks and mandatory lift evidence. Every motion costs action steps.
Returns plan_ok, plan_fail_reason, stages, release_commanded, retention and optional localization/route/pick_exit diagnostics; grasp_verified:false and placement_verified:false. Failure after opening can retain release_commanded:true.
Depth and surface matching cannot establish identity, seating, vacancy, unseen clearance or reachability; candidates never execute automatically. checked_pick covers only vertical exit; checked_transfer also guards loaded transit and descent.
