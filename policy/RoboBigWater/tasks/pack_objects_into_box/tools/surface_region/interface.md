`robo surface_region --u0 U --v0 V --u1 U --v1 V [--camera head --z_min Z --z_max Z --height H --footprint_x X --footprint_y Y --landing_z L]`
Read-only calibrated depth geometry in an inclusive pixel rectangle; no motion or action-budget cost. Camera: head|wrist_l|wrist_r; pixels use original image dimensions; optional z limits filter absolute height in metres.
Returns plan_ok, plan_fail_reason, sample_count, valid_fraction, bounds_min/max/center, surface_median, highest_point/pixel, z_percentiles_05_50_95, elongation and length_heading_mod180 (degrees from +x toward +y).
Heading is null for sparse/nearly round data and has 180° ambiguity; it does not identify front/back. Selected background and occluders contribute to all geometry.
With z_min and enough elongated data, returns up to three grasp_candidates: surface_point/pixel, opening_heading_mod180, visible_width_m, center_gap_m and sample_count; otherwise [].
Candidates are measured visible top samples, not TCP insertion heights; headings are perpendicular to visible length; width excludes hidden geometry and center_gap_m measures distance from the strip center. Retention is unverified.
Supplying footprint_x,footprint_y,landing_z together returns up to three landing_candidates ranked by lowest max_z: center_xy, min_z, max_z, height_span, checked_extent_xy, observed_cell_fraction. X/Y are footprint spans along x/y (.01–.50 m); centers describe footprints, not TCP targets.
Landing search requires every 5 mm cell, including a raster margin, to be observed at or below L; missing cells and higher samples block candidates regardless of z filters. Empty means no observed fit. Selection defines the searched region; no hidden-support, path, containment or landing guarantee; spans over 2 m fail.
Height explicitly assumes H metres and intersects camera rays without depth; returns corner_points, center_point, bounds_min/max and xy_change_per_cm_height instead of surface statistics. It is incompatible with z filters or landing arguments and does not measure height.
Fails on invalid/nonfinite arguments, calibration or rectangle; unavailable depth without height; no valid samples; or backward/parallel rays.
