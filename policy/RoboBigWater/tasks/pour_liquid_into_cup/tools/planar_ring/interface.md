`robo planar_ring --points '[[u,v],...]' [--base_points '[[u,v],...]' --support_z Z | --plane_z Z] [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--plane_error .01] [--pixel_error 2]`
Free, read-only reconstruction of a horizontal circular boundary from 6–64 distinct image landmarks covering at least 240°.
`base_points` adds 6–64 landmarks on a circular support-contact edge covering at least 120°; a visible front arc is sufficient. Exclude shadows, handles and side-wall markings.
Base mode fits the contact circle on the broad depth support plane, then recovers upper height and radius from the shared vertical axis; the two radii may differ. Without explicit `plane_z`, a >20 mm low bias versus regional depth triggers independent depth reconstruction of height, centre and radius, reported as `regional_boundary_depth_recovery` with the original `support_arc_candidate`; >30 mm depth spread fails instead.
`support_z` optionally supplies that plane in world metres; disagreement >12 mm with observed support fails. Omitted means measured support; base mode requires a measurable broad plane.
Without `base_points`, `plane_z` fixes height; omitting both estimates height from median 5×5 boundary-depth patches on the observed support plane's foreground. With both, disagreement >12 mm fails with `ring_height_conflict` and no executable geometry.
`plane_error` (.002–.03 m) controls explicit-plane perturbations; depth mode uses at least max(20 mm, two robust deviations), rejecting >30 mm spread; base mode uses ±4 mm support perturbations. `pixel_error` (.5–5 px) controls coherent outline perturbations.
Returns centre, radius, height source, residuals, coverage, selection sensitivity and `cycle_geometry` containing `rim_x/rim_y/rim_z/rim_radius` plus `rim_height_margin=max(.03, height sensitivity)` for execution clearance; this allowance is not a certified error bound.
Assumes horizontal circles; base mode also assumes coaxial cross-sections and actual support contact. No attachment or containment verification; sensitivity is not an accuracy guarantee.
Regional boundary-depth cross-check requires ≥70% valid landmark patches. Explicit height below their median by more than max(20 mm, two robust standard deviations) fails with `ring_depth_height_conflict` and no `cycle_geometry`; explicit heights are never replaced. Recovery retains depth uncertainty and the same excessive-sensitivity rejection.
Fails with `plan_ok=false` and `plan_fail_reason` for invalid inputs, missing data, conflicting height, inconsistent geometry, inadequate coverage or excessive sensitivity.
