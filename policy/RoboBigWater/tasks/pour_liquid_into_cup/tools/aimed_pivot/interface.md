## Tool: aimed_pivot
`robo aimed_pivot <arm> --x X --y Y --z Z --angle DEG --target_x X --target_y Y --target_z Z --source_z Z --dir_x X --dir_y Y --dir_z Z --speed_min V --speed_max V --target_radius R --source_radius R --position_error E --direction_error DEG --extent M --radius M --support_z Z [pivot rotation, finish, clearance and obstacle options]`
Selects a ballistic source position with trajectory_aim, rejects a negative target_margin_m before motion, then executes pivot transfer=arc to that position. No automatic height change or retry.
Current attached reference is (x,y,z); target is a horizontal disk; source_z is the chosen final reference height. No to_* or transfer arguments. All distances meters, speeds m/s, angles degrees, coordinates world-frame.
Direction is the normalized nominal emission vector at the final requested angle; direction_error bounds uncertainty around it. The tool adds the planned destination rotation/reversal sweep using rigid attachment. Source_radius bounds horizontal source width; position_error bounds XY error. Supplied bounds are estimates.
Requires the trajectory_aim arguments and ranges, including source_z above target_z, explicit width/position/angular bounds, and speed interval. Uses that tool's gravity-only conservative footprint model.
Requires pivot extent/radius/support_z; accepts its axis, frame, step, hold, finish, clearance, six obstacle bounds and tcp_radius, with unchanged defaults, validation and tracking stops. No full-scene collision guarantee.
Returns pivot feedback, landing_checked=true after footprint acceptance, plus aiming (source_position, target_margin_m, destination_sweep_deg, uncertainty bounds and max_fitting_source_z). Height bounds assume the reported cone and recenter reserve; changed heights require new clearance/aiming checks. Invalid aiming or trajectory_footprint stops before motion; pivot failures may leave partial motion.
Aiming applies only at the final source position; discharge during transit, slip, volume and completion are unverified. A successful bound is conditional on supplied estimates. Motions/holds cost action steps; gripper stays unchanged.
