## Tool: settled_cycle
`robo settled_cycle <left|right> --x X --y Y --z Z --ref_x X --ref_y Y --ref_z Z --rim_x X --rim_y Y --rim_z Z --rim_radius R --extent M --radius R --support_z Z [--peak DEG] [--hold SEC] [--gap M]`
Executes a forward axial grasp, automatic clearance lift, upright alignment and near-side lowering rotation, deep rotation/dwell and fixed-XY return; gripper remains closed. All motions consume action steps.
World coordinates in meters: x/y/z are contact TCP; ref_* is the attached upper axial point BEFORE grasp; rim_* and rim_radius describe a measured raised circular opening; support_z is the measured support plane.
Extent is full axial length below ref (.08.. .35); radius bounds the attached body (.005.. .08); rim_radius .015.. .12. Geometry must come from observations; initial shape is upright with reference above contact and XY separation <=.015.
Defaults: peak 135 degrees (125..135), hold 3.5 seconds (3..4), gap .04 m (.02.. .04). Rotation is +world-y for left, -world-y for right; waypoint spacing <=15 degrees.
Reference aligns over rim center while upright, then lowers with a continuous cylinder clearance bound and a mirrored near-side inset rising to rim_radius/2 at 90 degrees and retained at peak; gap is a minimum height above rim. Return retains dwell XY and reverses the interpolation-checked clearance heights; pauses .6 s at 60 degrees.
Returns plan_ok/plan_fail_reason, phase reports, measured TCP-derived tilt history, reached_reference and best-effort blue table patch candidates before/after dwell and after return. Episode termination returns plan_ok=false/episode_over and task_success_verified=false, without identifying the end reason. Rigid attachment/slip and actual completion are unverified; no patches does not prove containment.
Rejects invalid geometry before grasp; stops at first planning failure, workspace clipping, >.002 m reference error, >1 degree orientation error or episode end. Partial execution leaves the grasp closed; no blind retries, release or homing. Clearance excludes handles, hand volume and unseen obstacles.
