# Measured placement playbook

Final v0.2 development records: 9/10 passed (standard 4/5, random 5/5), across evolving tools; not a final-version sweep.
Rank 1 means smallest. All placements must be upright, with increasing world x and aligned y.

1. Read `obs`, then `color_geometry --color surface --camera head`; compare RGB with depth heights, widths and circular sections. Separate clutter by appearance and geometry; hue alone is insufficient.
2. Remeasure unresolved/occluded centers after clearing an empty hand or changing view. Use measured support; successful estimates ranged .7655–.775 m, not a fixed table constant.
3. Plan a reachable row once, preserving already useful placements. Space centers for measured bodies AND the hand envelope; shift the row if reach/occupancy checks reject it.
4. Convert a measured `body_contact` or circular section through `contact_pose` with the intended arm, approach and opening axis. A section center is not a TCP pose; a candidate still needs scene/reach checks.
5. Use free `transfer_plan` to check candidates before spending commands. Execute `guarded_transfer` with matching parameters; execution recomputes current checks. Recompute contact geometry after changing orientation.
6. Prefer one checked transfer per moved body. If direct reach fails, use a clear table staging point reachable by both arms; withdraw, clear the first hand, remeasure and regrasp with the other arm.
7. On preflight rejection, distinguish occupancy, opposite-hand interference, visibility and IK. Reposition an empty hand, revise the target/orientation/opening axis or stage; do not repeatedly execute an unchanged rejection.
8. On any motion/lift failure, inspect stage flags, reached TCP and head/wrist views. A closed hand may hold the payload; do not restart an open-gripper sequence or home blindly. Never carry after an unconfirmed grasp.
9. Lower and withdraw vertically, clearing the released top before lateral motion/home. Remeasure after drift or staging; verify standing height and row order independently because `placement_verified=false` even on completion.
10. Track both budgets: 60 commands and 1,050 action steps (42 s at 25 Hz). Free perception/planning still costs wall time. Finish with empty arms home after checking the row.

Successful settings were measured per scene: clearance usually .04 m, sometimes .05–.15; margin .005–.012; verification radius .020–.065; payload radius .017–.065.
Both down and down45 worked, usually open=x; open=y resolved some cluttered hand-sweep conflicts. Auto tries both approaches only before motion. Direct and auto routes both succeeded.
Equal source/destination TCP z worked on the same support. Radius and margin changes need geometric evidence; shrinking a footprint to bypass occupancy is not a validated remedy.
