`robo grasp_at ARM --x X --y Y --z Z [--open x|y] [--clearance C] [--height H] [--tolerance E] [--separation S]`
Vertical approach to an absolute TCP point, close, lift to Z+C (or H); ARM is left or right; head depth rejects Z more than 8 mm above or 6 mm below a resolved nearby horizontal surface, returning surface_z/suggested_tcp_z without motion.
`robo place_at ARM --x X --y Y --z Z [--ox OX --oy OY --oz OZ] [--clearance C] [--height H] [--finish release|hover] [--offset_mode manual|aperture] [--target_mode cap|point] [--refine auto|required|off] [--tolerance E] [--separation S]`
XYZ is a held feature’s final world position; manual mode (default) uses OX,OY,OZ as feature-minus-current-TCP (default zero); aperture mode measures the wrist opening offset, requires zero explicit offsets, and fails before motion if unavailable; orientation is preserved.
Release defaults to target_mode=cap: current depth must resolve one flat circular or rounded spherical cap within 12 mm XY and Z-2 to Z+60 mm; its axis replaces XY, preserving Z; unavailable/ambiguous fits stop before motion. point uses literal XYZ; hover skips this check and stops above XYZ closed.
Grasp transit height defaults to Z+C; placement defaults to max(current TCP Z, Z-OZ+C); H overrides this and must be at least final TCP Z+C.
Before motion, the entire TCP route must stay S=0.12 m [0.04,0.25] from the other TCP; failure other_arm_route_proximity returns arm_clearance with other_arm, other_tcp, minimum_m and required_m. This does not model arm links or obstacles; C must clear surfaces and held geometry.
Meters: C=0.10 [0.02,0.25], H=0 means automatic, E=0.008 [0.001,0.02]; offset norm <=0.2.
Returns target_check (axis fit or failure), held_feature (automatic wrist opening measurement after lift, or unavailable), applied_offset, height_check, motion_summary, stages, transit_z, released, plan_ok/plan_fail_reason; unavailable grasp depth leaves Z unchanged; grasp and seating verification remain false.
After transit, refine=auto remeasures the wrist opening and applies offsets within 5 mm of the input with circle/plane residuals <=0.4/0.3 mm; unavailable fits retain the input. required stops closed on unavailable fits; off skips refinement. transport_check reports the outcome; adjusted routes are checked again.
Each call uses one command; internal motion and gripper holds consume action steps.
