## Description

There is a cup containing many small balls and a vase. The robot needs to pick up the cup and pour all the balls into the vase.

## Scoring

| Score | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Not all balls are transferred into the vase. |
| 100 | All seven balls are inside the vase, the cup is upright, and the robot returns to origin. |
