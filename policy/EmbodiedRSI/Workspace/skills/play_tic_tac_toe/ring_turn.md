# Parameterized ring pickup, placement, and fixed-command handoff

Include `ee_motion.py` then `ring_turn.py`. The `ring_turn` helper composes the validated closed-loop moves with bounded close/release holds and one exact home action repeated throughout the opponent turn. Inputs specify arm, source and release EE xyz in world metres, release quaternion (scalar first), complete home action, remaining native budget, wait length and travel/clearance heights. Source and destination xyz and quaternion must be Python lists. The source grasp quaternion is the validated downward orientation (0.7071,0,0.7071,0). The returned report gives the last stage, total steps used, official success, and whether every planned stage completed. Official success may stop the helper early at release, leaving completed=false because later clearance/home stages were correctly skipped. Always inspect final images; completion of controller stages does not certify task success.

Preconditions: opponent confirmed parked, scene-dependent source and empty-cell targets calibrated, adequate native action budget, collision-free straight pose paths. Call separately per turn, reading the board after each opponent response. It is not safe to select all destinations ahead of the random opponent. For a pure vertical grasp use the pickup quaternion as placement quaternion; for a forward tilt provide the compensated release pose explicitly.

Stop conditions: exhausted caller-supplied budget, pose nontracking, episode termination or truncation. If stopped, inspect the stage and current grasp state before any recovery. Clearances must be reachable and above nearby pieces. The exact home action opens both grippers, so the inactive arm must not hold another object. The fixed wait has no opponent detector; extend it with the same home action if the current frame still shows the opponent moving.

Evidence: source grasp and placements in 000032-000043, and exact unchanged-home handoff in 000047-000048. The composed helper completed the officially successful attempt in 000054-000058 after placement coordinates were refined. Success occurred during the fifth release, at 982 of 1100 actions. Earlier visually full boards failed in 000044 and 000053; an opponent reply does not certify strict final placement tolerance. See lessons/placement_tolerance.md.
