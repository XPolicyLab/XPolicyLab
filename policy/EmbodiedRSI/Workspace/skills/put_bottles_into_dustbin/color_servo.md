# Color-centroid side alignment (experimental)
Uses only the left wrist RGB image and measured EE pose. Pink mask is r>150, g<100, b>65; assumes one unobstructed saturated pink bottle. The horizontal home orientation must be fixed, so camera image right is world +x and image up is world +z. align_pink_side adjusts world x/z in bounded increments, stopping on pixel tolerance, lost target, action cap, or termination. It does not advance toward the bottle or close the gripper. Initialize grip_targets for ee_control first. Pixel target and gain are parameters. Mask, gain and optical convention are scene-specific. Alignment validation is described below; no complete grasp or unseen-scene transfer claim is made.

Validation: 000088 converged to pixel (330.0,295.7) within 7 corrections after a horizontal staging pose. Desired orientation and standoff must be explicit; copying measured orientation in 000087 accumulated drift and did not converge. 000089 showed that image alignment alone does NOT validate approach depth: a 13 cm forward advance toppled the bottle. Use shorter incremental advances and inspect apparent object scale/contact before closure.

Final grasp-depth result: 000090 reproduced centroid convergence in 8 corrections. A shorter 6 cm advance preserved upright posture, but 000091-000092 showed the bottle slipping during the lift/carry and remaining on the table. This is a validated ALIGNMENT helper only, not a working complete pink-grasp skill. Do not claim bin placement from those observations.
