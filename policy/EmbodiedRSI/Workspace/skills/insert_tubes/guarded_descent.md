# Stop descent on contact or in-gripper slip
Use an upright tube held in a fixed-orientation wrist camera view, with a distinctive colored cap. `guarded_descent` takes arm, absolute target pose, grip command, camera name, rectangular pixel ROI, RGB bounds, action cap, downward increment, pixel motion threshold, and vertical residual threshold. The ROI/color mask must identify the held cap without background or other tubes. It compares the feature centroid before/after each downward action and stops on missing feature, slip, tracking residual, target height, budget, or episode end.

Evidence: prototype in 000058 stopped after ten 3 mm increments at a 12.6 pixel cap shift and 3.5 mm pose residual; the tube was still retained. This is more informative than EE tracking alone, which missed tube ejection in 000046 and 000050. Changes in pose orientation or lighting can move the centroid without slip. Caller must lift before planar correction and inspect/recalibrate the feature after contact. Tested only in this scene.

Validation update: 000059-000065 repeatedly stopped on cap motion while retaining the tube. The corrected left placement in 000065-000066 was accepted as part of official full success in 000068. ROI overlap with another tube's cap can bias the centroid; 000064 narrowed the ROI to exclude the previously placed right tube. Cap motion is a diagnostic trigger, not proof of failure: near final seating, some slip can occur while the rack correctly supports the tube.
